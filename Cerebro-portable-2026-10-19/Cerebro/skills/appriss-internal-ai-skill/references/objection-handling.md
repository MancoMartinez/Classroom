# Objection Handling Reference

Know these cold. Every answer below is designed to be said out loud — not just understood intellectually.

---

## "How do I know your AI is actually good and not just AI washing?"

> "You're right. Here's how to pressure-test any AI claim — ask three questions:
> 1. Is it trained on the full picture of my consumers and operations?
> 2. Does the AI make decisions or display data?
> 3. Can you query the data and have it take actions in your workflow?
>
> We pass all three. Most competitors pass one at best. They may be able to make decisions or they may even have some agentic capabilities, but it's always operating on incomplete data — making any output inaccurate."

**Quick version:** "Our AI is live and we can show you in the platform — live return authorization, live case linking, Sidekick assigning a work item. Right now."

---

## "Can you show me the AI in action?"

> "We can show you our AI live in the platform — not a productionized demo. Live return authorization, live case linking, the Sidekick workflow. It's not marketing language. It's in use by all of our customers."

---

## "Is this just a ChatGPT wrapper?"

> "No. A ChatGPT wrapper doesn't understand the complexity of your retail data or have the total loss context. Identity resolution, case linking, return authorization — these are purpose-built systems trained on our data for 20+ years. Where we use GenAI/Agentic, it's deeply integrated. Sidekick queries your actual database and takes actions in your workflow."

**Longer version if pressed:**
> "External LLMs are used by Sidekick to understand what the user is asking — natural language intent parsing. But every decision that matters to your business — return authorization, fraud scoring, case linking — uses fully proprietary models that Appriss Retail built, trained, optimized, and owns. ChatGPT has no knowledge of what a retail investigator is looking for or how to handle a return fraud pattern."

---

## "Is your AI auditable and explainable?"

> "Yes — across Engage, Secure, and Incident, every AI-driven decision is fully auditable.
>
> In Engage, we show exactly which factors drove any return outcome — critical for customer service escalations, LP investigations, and regulatory requirements.
>
> In Secure, alerts include the underlying signals — transaction patterns, behavioral indicators, identity links — so investigators understand what triggered a flag before they act.
>
> In Incident, case and investigation timelines are fully traceable. Every action, assignment, and escalation is logged for internal review or external compliance.
>
> And with Sidekick, every conversation is logged for governance and review. Sidekick never acts outside a user's existing permissions."

---

## "What about AI governance?"

> "Our AI governance program is formally structured. Our General Counsel holds the AI Governance Professional (AIGP) certification through IAPP — the leading credential in AI governance — bringing rigorous expertise in privacy, accountability, transparency, and ethics directly into how we build and oversee our AI systems. All employees complete mandatory AI Ethics and Compliance Training. If procurement or legal asks who owns AI governance at Appriss: our General Counsel, certified and accountable."

---

## "What about hallucinations?"

> "We use ML models for real-time decisions — those don't hallucinate. They score against structured data and return definitive, auditable answers with 99.99% decision accuracy. For the GenAI used in Sidekick — it's extensively tested and tuned to produce factual, workflow-relevant results. We use pre-trained models from one of the major model providers. No customer data is used to retrain these base models. As with any LLM, there's always a non-zero chance of error, which is why we always recommend a human-in-the-loop to validate outputs before acting on them."

---

## "How accurate are your models?"

> "Our models have delivered 99.99% decision accuracy for 20 years — and just as importantly, they're built to minimize false positives. We protect retailers without punishing good customers. Competitors who over-index on fraud detection create customer friction, and that has its own cost. What makes this possible is the training data: 20+ years of retail transaction data — billions of return authorizations. The breadth and depth of that signal is what separates us."

---

## "Our POS system is unusual — can your AI handle that?"

> "Our Data Intelligence layer normalizes data from every retailer into a common data model. Every retailer sends data differently — our foundation handles the translation so our ML models and analytics work consistently, regardless of what systems your customer uses at the POS."

---

## "We already have an analytics solution."

> "Most analytics tools only show you part of the picture — they're working from whatever data they have access to. Our Data Intelligence foundation pulls from 20+ years of retail transaction data, which means when we show you what happened, it's the most complete view available. But we don't stop at showing you. Our AI decides what happens next — return authorization, case linking, outlier detection. The question isn't whether you have analytics. It's whether your analytics has the full picture, and whether your AI is making decisions for you — or waiting for you to make them yourself."

---

## "Where is your AI going on the roadmap?"

> "We're moving from AI that responds to AI that initiates. Today, Sidekick helps analysts when they ask. Tomorrow, a fully agentic AI monitors data streams, detects anomalies, creates work items, links related incidents, and drafts investigation summaries — proactively, without a human triggering each step. Human-in-the-loop remains a core part of our design philosophy."

**Important:** Share directionally. Never commit to timelines or delivery dates.

## "How do you handle data privacy and compliance?"

> "All proprietary ML models are trained on Appriss Retail's own historical transaction and return data — we do not train models on PII. Cross-retailer risk signals are anonymized and aggregated before sharing across the network — no retailer's customer data or PII is exposed to another retailer. Sidekick's LLM features use pre-trained models; no customer data is used to retrain those base models. All customer data is processed in accordance with our data processing agreements and applicable data residency requirements."

---

## "Is Sidekick free?"

> "All Sidekick capabilities currently in beta are provided free of charge as part of the beta program. This has been clearly communicated. Going forward, the plan is to offer some capabilities as a free tier — natural language query building is likely to be included, potentially with usage limits for high-volume users. A formal monetization strategy for heavy users is being developed. For now: the beta is free."

---

## "How does Sidekick handle data access — can it see everything?"

> "Sidekick supports four configuration levels, so different organizations can adopt it at a pace that matches their compliance and data governance requirements — from Level 1, where the LLM has zero access to retailer data, all the way to Level 4, which includes transaction-level data. Retailers choose which level to grant, and Sidekick never acts outside a user's existing platform permissions. You can start at Level 1 or 2 with no sensitive data touched, and expand as your comfort and compliance framework allows."
