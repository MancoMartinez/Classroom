To answer your question, our engineering team confirmed that assigning a hierarchy to a new user is an administrator task. The platform does not assign one when a user account is created, so each new user needs at least one hierarchy assignment before they can select a store and start a case.

The steps you followed for Gabriel are the correct process. From Settings > Users, select the user, click Edit and open the Hierarchies tab. Choose Standard Location Hierarchy, then the level that matches the user's scope, such as Location for a single store or District for a whole district. Pick the store or district under Select Locations, click Add, and then click Save at the top of the page. The assignment shows up under Members on the same tab, and the user can select that location the next time they sign in. I have attached two screenshots of the Hierarchies tab for reference.

It may help to add this step to your onboarding checklist for new users, so nobody gets stuck on their first case. If any user still cannot select a location after a hierarchy has been assigned, just reply here and I will look into that account directly.
