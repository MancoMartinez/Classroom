1. Does pyardm cause a 1-day delay?
- pyardm doesn't shift dates. tradingday is the file's own transaction date.
- pyardm only loads a batch when all four POS file types for that date are present: tlogjda, TRE_TranHeader, TRE_TranDetail and TRE_TranTender. Anything
incomplete waits in the
landing folder.
- In practice pyardm waits for the tlog. The TRE files land at about 07:20 and the tlog at about 22:10, roughly 15 hours later.
- SnowTime builds the TRE data as soon as it lands. That's the "one day sooner" the client sees.
2. What's fine
- Items don't get orphaned. ARDM_ITEM rows with no header number 0 for every tradingday from 09-28 to 10-07.
- Header and detail transaction
IDs agree, because the header, detail and tender files land within about 30
seconds of each
other.
- Headers have one transaction ID per natural key, so there are no duplicate headers.
3. What's wrong
- department and taxamount on TRE-sourced item rows come from a tlog join in map_ardm_item. The tlog isn't in the window when the 07:20 fire runs.
- Prod for tradingdays 10-01 to 10-07 shows about 100% null department and about 100% zero taxamount on TRE rows.
- In a lab window where both feeds were present, the same columns were 5.1% null and 22% zero. So the cause is window membership, not bad mapping. About 5% stay null even when both
feeds are present.
- I haven't established when this started.
4. Inventory delay
The customer's proposed 8-hour delay for the inventory feed is fine. The inventory feeds only touch reference_product. costofgoodssold, which could be about 8 hours staler. They're i
part of the item department and taxamount path.
