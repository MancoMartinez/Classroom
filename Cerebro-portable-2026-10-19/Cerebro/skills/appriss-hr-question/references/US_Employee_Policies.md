## Time Off, Hours, and Attendance Policy

At Appriss Retail, we believe that time away from work and fair compensation practices are essential for employee well-being and organizational success. This policy outlines expectations for attendance, pay practices, time tracking, and all forms of paid and unpaid leave, ensuring compliance with federal, state, and local laws.

- **Reliability:** Employees are expected to be present and ready to work as scheduled. Notify your manager promptly if you will be late, absent, or need to leave early.
- **Notification:** Contact your manager before each day of absence. Failure to notify for three consecutive workdays may be treated as voluntary resignation (where legally permissible).
- **Time Recording:** Hourly employees must accurately record start/end times, meal breaks, and any personal departures in Rippling. Altering or falsifying time records may result in termination.
- **Meal and Rest Breaks:** Meal Break: 30-minute unpaid break for shifts over 6 hours (fully relieved of duty). Rest Break: 10-minute paid break for every 4 hours worked. State-specific rules apply.
- **Overtime:** Non-exempt employees working over 40 hours/week are paid at 1.5x regular rate. Paid leave does not count toward overtime. Overtime requires prior manager approval.
- **Paydays:** Semi-monthly. Mandatory deductions and authorized voluntary deductions apply. Pay stubs and W-2 forms are available in Rippling.

### Results Only Work Environment (ROWE)

Salaried employees do not accrue PTO. These employees have the flexibility to take time off as needed upon approval from their direct manager.

ROWE rules:
- Plan time off with your manager; time will be paid
- You are responsible for managing your workload and maintaining performance standards
- ROWE is intended to build trust. If performance declines due to abuse, the Company may review usage and determine if discipline is appropriate
- ROWE does not apply to unpaid leaves of absence; you cannot extend a paid leave with ROWE
- If an absence under ROWE lasts 3+ business days and qualifies as a leave of absence, leave terms apply
- Manager discretion does not apply to legally protected leaves (paid sick leave, other job-protected time off)

### Special Leave Categories

- **Bereavement:** Up to 5 paid days for immediate family; 1 day for extended family
- **Jury Duty:** Paid up to 2 weeks; provide documentation
- **Voting Time:** Reasonable paid time if work schedule conflicts with voting hours
- **Domestic Violence Leave:** Employees may use PTO, PSL, or unpaid leave for matters related to domestic violence, sexual assault, or stalking, including medical attention, shelter services, counseling, safety planning, relocation, and legal proceedings. Job protection and confidentiality guaranteed under applicable state laws.

### Compliance and Non-Retaliation

All time off related to protected reasons (sick leave, kin care, domestic violence, jury duty, voting) is job-protected. The Company will not retaliate against employees for exercising rights under this policy.

## Leave of Absence Policy – 2026

The Company provides several types of leave to support employees during times of personal, family, or medical need.

### 1. Overview of Leave Types

- **Family and Medical Leave (FMLA)** – Federally mandated job-protected leave
- **State-Specific Leave Programs** – Mandated by the employee's state of residence
- **Short-Term Disability (STD)** – Company-sponsored wage replacement
- **Paid Family Leave (PFL)** – Company-sponsored wage replacement for bonding/care
- **Other Leaves** – Bereavement, jury duty, personal leave, and workers' compensation are managed separately

**NOTE:** All leaves follow the same general process for requesting, approval, pay, and reinstatement.

### 2. Family and Medical Leave Act (FMLA)

#### 2.1 Purpose

Ensures eligible employees receive up to 12 weeks of job-protected leave in a 12-month period for qualifying family and medical reasons.

#### 2.2 Eligibility

Employees who have worked for the Company for at least 12 months (not necessarily consecutive) and have worked at least 1,250 hours during the 12-month period immediately before the leave.

#### 2.3 Reasons for Leave

Up to 12 workweeks of unpaid leave per 12-month period for:
- Birth or care of a child
- Adoption or foster placement
- Care for a family member with a serious health condition
- The employee's own serious health condition
- Qualifying military exigencies
- Up to 26 weeks to care for a covered servicemember with a serious injury or illness

#### 2.4 Leave Period

The Company uses a rolling 12-month period measured backward from the date the leave is used.

#### 2.5 Intermittent or Reduced Schedule Leave

Employees may take intermittent or reduced schedule leave when medically necessary. The Company may temporarily transfer employees to an alternate position; any transfer will not reduce pay or benefits.

#### 2.6 Substitution of Paid Leave

Employees may use accrued paid leave concurrently with unpaid FMLA leave.

#### 2.7 Notice Requirements

Employees must provide 30 days' notice when foreseeable; otherwise, as soon as practicable.

#### 2.8 Leave Administration

Mutual of Omaha administers all FMLA leaves, including verification, certification, and tracking. People Ops coordinates internally.

#### 2.9 Medical Certification

Employees must provide medical certification for their own or a family member's serious health condition within the required timeframe. A fitness-for-duty certification may be required for return.

#### 2.10 Benefits During FMLA Leave

Group health insurance continues during FMLA leave on the same terms as active employment. Employees must continue to pay their share of premiums.

#### 2.11 Job Restoration

Employees returning from FMLA leave are reinstated to the same or equivalent position with equivalent pay, benefits, and conditions, unless the position was eliminated for business reasons.

#### 2.12 Protections

Employees are protected from retaliation or interference for requesting or taking FMLA leave.

#### 2.13 Coordination with Other Leave Policies

FMLA runs concurrently with other applicable leaves unless prohibited by law.

#### 2.14 Policy Administration

People Operations administers this policy. Contact People Operations with questions.

## Progressive Discipline Policy

### Purpose

Appriss Retail's progressive discipline policy provides a structured corrective action process to improve and prevent a recurrence of undesirable employee behavior and performance issues.

Appriss Retail reserves the right to combine or skip steps depending on the facts of each situation. The steps below are guidelines only and do not establish a required sequence or prerequisite to disciplinary action or termination.

**Nothing in this policy provides any contractual rights regarding employee discipline or counseling, nor should anything in this policy be read as modifying or altering the employment-at-will relationship between Appriss Retail and its employees.**

### Procedure

**Step 1: Counseling and Verbal Warning**

The immediate supervisor brings attention to the existing performance, conduct, or attendance issue. The supervisor discusses the nature of the problem, clearly describes expectations, and outlines the steps the employee must take to improve.

**Step 2: Written Warning**

More-formal documentation of the issues and consequences. The immediate supervisor, a member of the People Ops team, and a division manager or director (optional) meet with the employee. A formal Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) will be issued within five business days of a Step 2 meeting. The written warning may include a statement that additional discipline, up to and including termination, may follow if corrective action is not taken.

**Step 3: Recommendation for Termination of Employment**

The most serious step. Appriss Retail will generally try to exercise the progressive nature of this policy before proceeding to termination. However, Appriss Retail reserves the right to combine and skip steps based on circumstances.

Management's recommendation to terminate must be approved by People Operations and the department director or designate. Final approval may be required from the CEO or a designated officer.

### Performance and Conduct Issues Not Subject to Progressive Discipline

Illegal behavior, theft, substance abuse, intoxication, fighting, and other acts of violence at work are not subject to progressive discipline and may result in immediate termination. Such behavior may be reported to local law enforcement authorities.

### Documentation

Employees will receive copies of all progressive discipline documentation, including all PIPs, and may be asked to sign copies attesting to receipt and understanding. Copies are placed in the employee's official personnel file.
