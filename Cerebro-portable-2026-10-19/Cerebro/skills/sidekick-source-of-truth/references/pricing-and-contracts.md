# Sidekick — Pricing & Contracts

## Tiers at a glance

| Tier | Price/year | User count | Annual escalator |
|---|---|---|---|
| Essential | $15,000 | Under 50 users | 10% |
| Pro | $45,000 | 50–499 users | 10% |
| Premier | $150,000 | 500+ users | 10% |

All tiers: three-year recurring contracts. 10% escalator is shown upfront (not a surprise).

**Example TCV — Essential:** $15K → $16,500 → $18,150 = **$49,650 total contract value**

Credits are granted at the start of each contract year. Use-it-or-lose-it at renewal (no rollover).

---

## Credit model

Consumption-based — not per seat. Credit cost scales with action complexity:
- Low end: single-turn Q&A, simple navigation, search composition
- High end: overnight agent runs, scheduled reports, deep analyses

Tier sizing is built around realistic usage patterns:
- **Essential:** ~24 users × 20 conversations/day (96% GM)
- **Pro:** ~150 users × 12 conversations/day (95% GM)
- **Premier:** ~400 users × 20 conversations/day (93% GM)

Custom tier available above Premier — floor of $0.075 per credit, negotiated.

---

## Credit limit behavior

- **At 80% utilization:** Internal alert fires automatically → CSM initiates replenishment conversation
- **At 100% utilization:** Second alert fires. Access not immediately cut off, but upsell/continuation needs to happen with urgency.
- Additional credits are pro-rated to next renewal date — everything expires on the same cycle. No stranded balances.

---

## Tier flexibility / upsell math

No discounting. Tier combinations are the flexibility mechanism:

| Starting tier | Upsell to | Combined price |
|---|---|---|
| Essential ($15K) | Two Essential tiers | $30K |
| Pro ($45K) | Pro + Essential | $60K |
| Premier ($150K) | Premier + Essential | $165K |

There is plenty of room between tiers — selling up is always the move.

---

## Why three-year, not pay-as-you-go

Predictability for both sides. The 10% escalator is visible upfront so customers see exactly what the next three years cost. Annual commit removes the adoption brake that comes with variable bills — teams can use Sidekick fully without worrying about an unpredictable invoice.

## Getting a customer started — four steps

1. **Surface interest.** Email Carly (Carly Erickson, Sr. PM).
2. **Scope config level.** Product loops in with the customer's compliance team to pick Level 1–4.
3. **Sign.** Use pricing models to drive sign-on conversation and approvals.
4. **Onboard immediately.** Flip the switch; Sidekick is active. Documentation and prompt library ready to send.

**Pre-call checklist:**
- Know which configuration level the customer is most likely to be comfortable with
- Have one specific use case ready mapped to the buyer's role
- Be ready to demo live: search composer, work item summary, ad-hoc trend analysis
- Lead with the work, not the technology
- Never commit to roadmap timelines
