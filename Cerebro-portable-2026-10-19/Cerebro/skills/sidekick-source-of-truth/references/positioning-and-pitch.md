# Sidekick — Positioning & Pitch

## Positioning statement

For loss prevention and operations teams who work inside the Appriss platform every day, Sidekick is the embedded collaborator that turns the platform into a workflow engine your team can talk to. Compose searches, pull profiles, summarize investigations, generate analyses, take actions, and run reports — all in plain language. Built into Engage, Secure, and Incident. Using the same permissions your team already operates within today.

## The category we're leading

Appriss organizes its intelligence into three layers. Sidekick is the third:

- **Data Intelligence** — 20+ years of cross-retailer transaction history, 250M consumer IDs, identity resolution that follows the customer across cards, channels, and addresses.
- **Decision Intelligence** — Real-time return scoring, shrink anomaly detection, case linking. 12+ purpose-built systems.
- **Workflow Intelligence** — Sidekick. The part your team talks to.

**When a buyer asks "what is Sidekick?" — answer:** "Sidekick is the workflow intelligence layer of the Appriss platform. It's the part your team talks to."

## The pitch — three versions

### 15-second (hallway, trade show)
"Sidekick is the workflow intelligence layer of the Appriss platform. It's the part your team talks to. Your team types what they want — build a search, pull a profile, summarize an investigation, assign a work item — and Sidekick goes and does it. Embedded in Engage, Secure, and Incident."

### 30-second (intro call, follow-up)
"Some of the work your loss prevention team does every day is platform navigation — clicking through filters, hunting menus, copying context between cases. Sidekick replaces that with conversation. Your team types what they need; Sidekick goes and does it inside the same product they already use. Composing searches, pulling profiles, summarizing open investigations, running analyses, taking actions like assigning work items. Same data, same permissions, just faster. In beta, an LP leader who used to spend 45 minutes prepping for a store visit now just asks Sidekick — 'I'm visiting store 22 today, what should I talk to the manager about?' — and has the answer in 5."

### 2-minute (discovery, demo intro, executive briefing)
"Look at how your LP and operations teams actually spend their day. Most of it isn't analysis — it's figuring out where to start. Which search to run. Which case to look at. Which store to visit. The hard part isn't doing the work; it's knowing what work to do, and stitching the pieces together into something that actually tells you what's going on.

Sidekick changes that. Your team doesn't have to know what to search for anymore. They describe what they're trying to figure out — 'I'm visiting store 56 today, what should I know?' or 'Something feels off about this cashier, walk me through it' — and Sidekick connects the dots. It pulls the work items, the transactions, the profiles, the analyses, and stitches them into one narrative in seconds. Then it tells you what to act on.

It's built into Engage, Secure, and Incident. Same platform your team already uses. Same permissions. The only thing that changes is how fast your team gets to the right answer — and how much of the mental load they get back.

Most of the cost of an investigation isn't the click-through. It's the cognitive lift of holding ten threads in your head and figuring out which one matters. Sidekick takes that off your team. Investigators spend their time deciding, not searching. Analysts spend their time judging risk, not assembling context. District managers walk into stores already knowing what to address."

## Old vs. new framing

Use this on intro calls to anchor on the work, not the technology.

| The way it used to work | The way it works with Sidekick |
|---|---|
| Build a search by clicking through 12 filter menus | Type "show me cash transactions over $200 at store 104" |
| Open three tabs to compare cashier work items | Ask "do any of the cashiers at store 101 have work items I should look at?" |
| Stitch together a 45-minute investigation summary by hand | "Summarize work item 8346 with a timeline" — done in seconds |
| Configure an analysis, then wait for the next scheduled run | Run any analysis on demand against any result set |
| Train new analysts for weeks on platform navigation | Day-one productivity — they just type what they want |
| Tools live in separate tabs, separate logins, separate workflows | One conversation that works across Engage, Secure, and Incident |

## Value pillars

**Pillar 1 — Built into the work you already do**
"Sidekick lives inside Engage, Secure, and Incident. Not a separate tool. Not a separate login. Same product your team is already using." Accessible from any screen via ✨ sparkle icon, bottom-left corner.

**Pillar 2 — An active partner, not a passive answering machine**
"Most tools tell you what's there. Sidekick gets things done." Builds and runs searches, runs ad-hoc analyses, promotes one-off analyses to recurring work items, assigns work items, all inside the conversation.

**Pillar 3 — Inside your governance, not around it**
"Sidekick respects every control your team already has." Four configuration levels. Existing user permissions still apply. Every prompt and response logged. Customer data never used to train models. AIGP-certified governance lead.

## Cross-sell angle

Easy cross-sell hiding in plain sight: Secure-only customers who've adopted Sidekick. Every Appriss module they add makes Sidekick more useful — land Incident or Audit, and Sidekick immediately starts pulling cases and audit data into the same conversation it's already running on transactions. Most of these accounts use ThinkLP or Agilence for case management today, so it's displacement not entrenchment. Deal size is smaller than an Engage cross-sell but closes faster.
