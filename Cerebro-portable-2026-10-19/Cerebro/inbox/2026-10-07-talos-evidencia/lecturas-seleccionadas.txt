"ReturnPriceModifyCount / ReturnPriceModifyAmount. Filters: return_flag = 1, voided_flag = 0, manual_price_flag = 1. Purpose: Track manual price changes on returns."

Esta funcion, pre_insert_generate_fast_facts, corre en Postgres sobre ardm_item, ardm_itemdiscount y ardm_transactiondiscount antes de insertar en las tablas principales de ARDM.
