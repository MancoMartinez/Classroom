## 04 AI Framing Guidelines

*Grounded in buyer feedback from LinkedIn polls, AI survey, and enterprise customer calls (March 2026)*

**Theme 1 — Lead with data differentiation, not model count**
"The data no one else has — trillions in retail transactions reveal patterns invisible to any single retailer." That's the winning message. Lead with it.
- Do: "Built on 20+ years of retail data and 250M consumer IDs"
- Don't: "We have 12 AI models" (model count is table stakes, not differentiation)

**Theme 2 — Prove it, don't claim it**
Half of surveyed buyers are skeptical the moment they hear "AI-powered." Lead with outcomes tied to buyer goals.
- Do: "6x faster root cause identification" / "Declines fraudulent returns before they hit your P&L"
- Don't: "Our cutting-edge AI transforms your loss prevention strategy"

**Theme 3 — Specificity wins**
Capability-specific language tied to use cases is what buyers respond to.
- Do: "Behavioral pattern recognition. Real-time risk decisions. Natural language investigations."
- Don't: "AI-powered platform" / "Advanced machine learning"

**Theme 4 — Position as most mature, not as introduction**
75% of buyers are already running multiple AI vendors. They're comparing options — not learning what AI is.
- Do: Position Appriss as the most differentiated and proven option in the market
- Don't: Explain what AI does or treat them as AI newcomers

**Theme 5 — Equip the LP champion to sell upward**
LP leaders are rarely the final decision-maker. CFOs, FP&A, and Heads of Stores are.
- Always provide: ROI ratios, use-case examples, and business case language
- Frame for finance: margin %, EBITDA impact, payback period
- Frame for ops: handle time, training burden, process consistency
- Frame for CEO: shrink trend, loss as % of sales, brand protection

## 06 Content Guidelines

1. **Lead with business outcomes** (margin protection, profit, loyalty) — not features
2. **Frame as strategic profit protection** rather than reactive loss control
3. **Emphasize omnichannel unification and Data Intelligence** as the key differentiator
4. **Balance protection with experience** — never position as purely punitive
5. **Use "total retail loss platform" framing** to capture returns + shrink + incidents holistically
6. **Adapt depth to audience** — execs want strategic impact; practitioners want workflow benefits
7. **Include proof points** where credibility matters (60+ top 100, 40% U.S. transactions, 99.99% accuracy, 250M consumer IDs)
8. **Lead AI messaging with data** — the cross-retailer consortium and 20+ years of history is the differentiator, not model count
