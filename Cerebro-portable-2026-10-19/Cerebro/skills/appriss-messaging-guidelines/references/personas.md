## LP / Asset Protection Manager

*Day-to-day investigator*

**LEAD WITH:** "Surface the high-value issues faster — so your team investigates what matters, not what the data team eventually gets back to you about. Sidekick answers your toughest questions in seconds."

**KEY PAIN:** Alert overload, manual digging, siloed data, limited headcount

**PROOF:** 6x faster root cause identification. 50% reduction in investigation time. Any analyst finds what used to require a power user.

**GOAL:** Detect, investigate, and resolve fraud, shrink, and cases quickly across channels.

**KPIs:** Cases opened/closed, time to investigation, $ recovered, fraud rings identified

**DECISION CRITERIA:** Accuracy of alerts, ability to surface high-value investigations, speed to insight.

**PRODUCTS:** Engage, Secure, Incident

## LP / AP VP or SVP

*Strategy owner — total loss*

**LEAD WITH:** "Full visibility into shrink and returns-related loss — addressed faster, so you can prove margin impact, justify the budget, and do more with the team you have."

**KEY PAIN:** Disconnected systems, difficulty proving ROI, rising loss and violence, no total loss picture

**PROOF:** 60+ top 100 retailers. Total-loss coverage across channels. Exec-level reporting. One trusted provider.

**GOAL:** Own shrink/fraud/safety strategy and total loss; align teams, tools, and budgets.

**KPIs:** Shrink %, total loss %, incident rates, fraud impact, cross-channel loss trends

**DECISION CRITERIA:** Total-loss coverage, ability to connect data across channels/teams, exec-level reporting.

**PRODUCTS:** Secure, Engage, Incident

## Finance Leader (CFO / VP Finance)

*Economic buyer — ROI and margin*

**LEAD WITH:** "Every time a fraudulent/abusive return hits your P&L or a cashier exception goes undetected, you're buying back loss you already paid for — before your team even knows it happened. Now you can get ahead of it and take back margin."

**KEY PAIN:** Diminishing profits, low visibility into loss sources, competing capital allocation

**PROOF:** Clear payback period. Margin/EBITDA impact. Declines fraudulent returns before P&L impact. LP team investigates more without adding headcount.

**GOAL:** Quantify loss and liability, protect margins and EBITDA, prioritize investments.

**KPIs:** Margin %, EBITDA impact, payback period, TCO vs current stack

**DECISION CRITERIA:** Clear, defensible ROI, margins/EBITDA impact, payback period, net-new revenue.

**PRODUCTS:** Engage, Secure

## Operations Leader (Store / Retail Ops)

*Executes in-store — consistency at scale*

**LEAD WITH:** "Quickly navigate shrink and returns with minimal coaching, easy AI-assisted workflows, no disruption to daily ops — the AI handles the triage, your team handles what matters."

**KEY PAIN:** High workload, constant turnover, lack of process consistency, multi-channel complexity

**PROOF:** Simple, low-training workflows. Automation minimizes conflict at the counter. Consistent process across hundreds of stores.

**GOAL:** Keep stores running smoothly while reducing shrink and handling returns efficiently.

**KPIs:** Queue times, handle time, training time, process adherence, shrink %

**DECISION CRITERIA:** Simple, low-training workflows. Automation minimizes conflict at the counter.

**PRODUCTS:** Engage, Secure, Incident, Coach

## Supply Chain & Reverse Logistics

*Returns recovery and cost owner*

**LEAD WITH:** "Gain visibility into returns and claims — move product faster, reduce leakage, and coordinate with LP and ecom so nothing falls through the cracks."

**KEY PAIN:** Volume spikes, slow processing, inconsistent data, limited LP/ecom connection

**PROOF:** Faster return routing. Reduced leakage in reverse flow. LP/ecom coordination in one platform.

**GOAL:** Improve how returned items are collected, moved, and processed at minimal cost.

**KPIs:** Days in reverse logistics, cost per unit, recovery value, leakage in reverse flow

**DECISION CRITERIA:** Return efficiency, visibility into item flow/leakage, LP/ecom coordination.

**PRODUCTS:** Engage, Secure, Incident

## Ecommerce / Digital Leader

*Digital revenue and CX owner*

**LEAD WITH:** "Accurately action on fraud, abuse, returns, and claims to protect margins while maintaining a stellar experience. Our Data Intelligence resolves consumer identity across every channel — keeping insult rates low while catching fraud and abuse your current tools miss entirely."

**KEY PAIN:** High online returns, growing fraud pressure, point solutions with narrow fraud-only mindset

**PROOF:** Omnichannel Consumer Identity Resolution. Low insult rate. Digital margin impact. Catches fraud that digital-only tools miss.

**GOAL:** Grow digital revenue profitably; manage online returns, claims, and fraud without hurting CX.

**KPIs:** Online return rate, claim rate, insult rate, digital margin per order

**DECISION CRITERIA:** Loss per return reduction, low insult rate, digital margin impact, omnichannel view.

**PRODUCTS:** Engage, Secure

## CTO / IT Leader

*Technical evaluator — architecture and integration*

**LEAD WITH:** "Reduce redundancies, satisfy store/ecom/LP/finance without adding IT burden — and an AI governance program you can actually show procurement. Our Data Intelligence layer normalizes data from every POS/OMS format so your team doesn't have to."

**KEY PAIN:** Integration complexity, limited IT resources, data reconciliation, too many vendors

**PROOF:** API compatibility. Data feed re-use. Security certifications. Vendor consolidation.

**GOAL:** Maintain secure, scalable architecture; navigate integrations and vendor sprawl.

**KPIs:** Time to implement, # integrations re-used vs net new

**DECISION CRITERIA:** API compatibility, data feed re-use, uptime/SLA, security certs, vendor consolidation.

**PRODUCTS:** Engage, Secure, Incident

## Customer Experience / Strategy Leader

*Loyalty and NPS owner*

**LEAD WITH:** "Reward your best customers while reducing loss — appreciate the best, mitigate the rest. Our AI knows who your loyal customers are across every channel, so good shoppers never pay the price for someone else's fraud or abuse."

**KEY PAIN:** Friction in returns, fear of losing market share, conflicting KPIs with LP/finance

**PROOF:** Configurable warnings vs. denials. Low insult rate. Reward good customers. Protect NPS alongside margin.

**GOAL:** Improve loyalty, NPS, and returns/claims perception while protecting profitability.

**KPIs:** NPS, return/denial complaints, escalation volume, warning vs denial outcomes

**DECISION CRITERIA:** Reward good customers, configurable warnings vs denials, low insult rate.

**PRODUCTS:** Engage

## CEO / Executive Leader

*Strategic oversight — brand and earnings*

**LEAD WITH:** "Shrink and returns are your two biggest unmanaged loss vectors. We give you one trusted view of both — and the AI to act on it before it hits earnings. That's strategic profit protection."

**KEY PAIN:** Macro-level risk, earnings volatility, no simple strategic view of total loss

**PROOF:** Shrink trend visibility. Loss as % of sales. Revenue at risk protected. One trusted provider — 60+ top 100 retailers.

**GOAL:** Protect top and bottom line; safeguard brand reputation; strategic oversight of risk.

**KPIs:** Shrink trend, loss as % of sales, revenue at risk protected

**DECISION CRITERIA:** Strategic impact (shrink %, margin), brand protection, one trusted provider.

**PRODUCTS:** Engage, Secure, Platform / Insights

## SVP of Transformation

*Enterprise change leader*

**LEAD WITH:** "Consolidate vendors, align teams, and show measurable ROI against shrink and returns-related loss without adding another change initiative."

**KEY PAIN:** Cross-functional complexity, change fatigue, many point solutions, hard to prove ROI

**PROOF:** Platform consolidation. Proven results. Clear KPI framework. Scalable across business units.

**GOAL:** Align teams across channels; drive measurable enterprise-wide transformation.

**KPIs:** Adoption across BUs, platforms consolidated, ROI vs business case

**DECISION CRITERIA:** Proven results, platform consolidation, clear KPI framework, scalable.

**PRODUCTS:** Engage, Secure, Platform / Insights
