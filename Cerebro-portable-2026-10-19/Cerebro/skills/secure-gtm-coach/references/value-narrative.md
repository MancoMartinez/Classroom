## 1. The Shrink Challenge

Shrink has a direct, 1-to-1 impact on margins — and most of it hides inside transactions that look completely normal.

**Scale of the Problem (2026 data):**
- $5.48T in total U.S. retail sales
- $90B lost to shrink annually
- $66B of that is preventable — 73% of total shrink
- $47B attributable to employee theft, ORC, and operational errors

Source: 2026 Total Retail Loss Benchmark Report, Appriss Retail

### Where the $66B Hides

Most retailers see the headline shrink number but can't decompose it. The loss breaks down as:

- **29% / $26B — Employee Theft:** Unauthorized voids, fake refunds, SKU substitutions, collusion schemes. Often the most damaging because it's deliberate, repetitive, and hard to see in any single transaction.
- **21% / $19B — Inventory Errors:** Distribution mispicks, transfer losses, receiving gaps. Vendor short-ships and over-ships that go unreconciled. Loss that looks like process variance until someone looks closely.
- **13% / $12B — Operational Inefficiencies:** Spoilage, coupon abuse, price manipulation. Often dismissed as "cost of doing business" because it doesn't look like theft.
- **10% / $9B — ORC:** Organized retail crime rings that exploit every gap between detection systems. They move between channels, locations, and schemes.

**The critical insight:** Most of this loss isn't flagged by current tools because it looks completely normal in any single-day view. The signal only emerges when you look at patterns — across associates, across time, across stores. That's exactly what rules-based systems can't do.

### Coaching Note

When presenting the shrink challenge, don't just recite the stats. Ask the retailer to tell you where they think their number comes from. Most LP leaders have a feel for theft, but they've never been able to decompose inventory shrink and operational errors the same way. That's the gap Secure closes — and helping the retailer see it for themselves is far more powerful than telling them about it.

Appriss Secure is the AI-driven shrink and exception analytics platform trusted by 60+ of the top 100 U.S. retailers. It ingests transaction, associate, inventory, and cash data from across the operation and surfaces what's statistically outside the norm — so LP teams can investigate the cases that matter and stop drowning in data.

**The tagline:** Stop shrink before it hits your margin.

**The three actions Secure enables:**
- **MONITOR:** Spot patterns before they cost you. One view of risk across every employee, location, product, and process.
- **INVESTIGATE:** Rank exceptions by risk level. Highest-value cases rise to the top, ready to act on.
- **CLEAR:** Every type of shrink, fraud, and operational error, caught. Workflows to investigate, coach, and close.

### How Secure Works

Four-step process:

1. **Ingest** — Pull transaction, associate, and operational data from every channel — POS, SCO, inventory, cash — into a single view.
2. **Configure** — Set up exception metrics tailored to your operations. Define the behaviors and thresholds that matter for your business — your team controls what the system looks for.
3. **Analyze** — Identify statistical outliers across your data. The system surfaces what's performing outside the norm — ranked by configured risk levels.
4. **Act** — Deliver clear work items: investigate, monitor, or clear. Your team acts on prioritized exceptions with full data context behind each one.

**Sidekick sits across configure, analyze, and act** — ask anything at any stage of your workflow.

### Key Capabilities (by Module)

**Exception-Based Reporting (EBR) — Core:**
Flags unusual POS and Ecom activity (high voids, suspicious discounts, return patterns) and scores every transaction against AI-trained outlier models. No thresholds to write. No rules to maintain. Patterns emerge from 20+ years of behavioral data across 60+ top 100 retailers. Search Composer lets analysts build custom queries across cashiers, stores, SKUs, and time periods in seconds. Every flagged exception generates a work item, investigation, or coaching session in one click.

Use cases: Cashier integrity · Sweethearting · Tender manipulation · Refund fraud · Coupon abuse · Discount override · SCO exceptions

**Secure Coach:**
Auto-generates a coaching task for a manager when a cashier's metrics cross a set threshold. Pinpoints associates with behavioral outliers needing correction — not punishment. Connects to POS, Cash Over/Short, and Inventory data. Tracks coaching completion and performance improvement over time.

**Cash Over/Short:**
Tracks cash drawer variances by store, register, and cashier — surfaces patterns over time. Captures paid-in/out, no-sale, and cash-back. One-screen Over/Short Summary. Cash Flips tab flags reversals. Real-time detection before the shift is over, not discovered in nightly reconciliation.

**Secure Inventory:**
Tracks receiving, transfers, and vendor deliveries to catch back-of-house discrepancies. Connects invoice data against actual shipments (DSD). Detects vendor short-ships, over-ships, recurring vendor errors, stockroom manipulation, and damage fraud. When paired with EBR, surfaces inventory patterns that neither tool can see alone.

**Sidekick:**
Conversational AI collaborator embedded in the Appriss platform. Works across Engage, Secure, and Incident — answers questions, surfaces insights, and takes actions. Plain-language queries against return data, exception reports, and incidents. Can build and run searches, pull up profiles, and assign work items. No report-building required.

## 6. Audience Track: The Current Secure Customer (Expansion)

**Audience:** Existing Secure customers — typically on Essential (EBR + Analytics) — who haven't yet added Coach, Cash Over/Short, Inventory, or aren't getting full value from what they have.

### The Expansion Motion

The module expansion path for Secure is: EBR (Essential) → Coach → Cash Over/Short → Inventory. Each module adds a new category of loss visibility that compounds the value of what's already deployed.

**The coaching principle for expansion:** Don't lead with the module. Lead with the category of loss they're currently blind to.

### Module-by-Module Expansion Narrative

**Adding Coach (most common first expansion):**

*The case:* Most LP teams using EBR can see which associates are outliers. They can build a case for disciplinary action. What they can't do — without Coach — is turn that insight into a documented, structured coaching conversation before it gets to that point. Coach closes that gap.

- Frame around: "You're already seeing the behavior. You're just not turning it into a coaching conversation at scale."
- Cost of inaction: Terminations that could have been avoided. Rehire costs ($5,000+ per associate). Lost productivity during onboarding.
- Proof: Top grocery retailer — $30M in savings, 46% decrease in hand-keyed items, 26% decrease in negative hand-keyed, 13% decrease in transaction voids, 10% decrease in coupon application.
- Ask: "How many associates has your LP team had to terminate in the last 6 months where, in hindsight, a coaching conversation earlier might have changed the outcome?"

**Adding Cash Over/Short:**

*The case:* EBR catches what happens at the register. But cash variance — paid-in/out fraud, no-sale manipulation, cash back abuse — lives in the drawer, and most retailers don't have visibility into register-level variance until nightly reconciliation. By then, the decision window is already closed.

- Frame around: "You're catching the transaction exceptions. But the cash side of the register is still a blind spot."
- Cost of inaction: Cash variance that looks like process error but is actually deliberate manipulation — accumulating across shifts before anyone sees the pattern.
- Proof: National grocery retailer replaced multi-query cash reconciliation with a single-screen Over/Short Summary. Corporate and field AP teams now monitor daily. Operational fixes reduced false positives and freed teams to focus on true theft.
- Ask: "How do you currently track cash variances? Is that happening real-time or next-day?"

**Adding Inventory (Ins/Outs & DSD):**

*The case:* EBR sees what happens at the register. Inventory sees what's happening in the back of the house — receiving, transfers, vendor deliveries. The shrink that comes from short-ships, receiving discrepancies, and stockroom manipulation is invisible until inventory and EBR are connected.

- Frame around: "Right now you can see exceptions at the register. But if the loss is happening before the product ever gets to the floor, EBR can't see it."
- Cost of inaction: Vendor discrepancies that go unreconciled, inventory adjustments that mask theft, damage fraud that looks like process inefficiency.
- Proof: 1.83% sustained lift in vendor credits per period at a national retailer. 0.23% sustained improvement in DSD working capital. 14 out-of-the-box risk searches ready to deploy on day one.
- Ask: "How are you currently reconciling vendor deliveries? What happens when a shipment comes in short?"

### Overcoming Expansion Stalls

The most common expansion stall is a customer who sees value in their current modules but hasn't connected the gap in what they're not seeing. The fix:

- **Quantify the undetected category.** If they don't have Inventory, estimate what vendor discrepancy loss looks like at their scale. If they don't have Cash O/S, ask how they'd know if register manipulation was happening and going undetected.
- **Use the SBR as the expansion vehicle.** The business review is the natural moment to show "here's what you're seeing now — here's what you'd be seeing with the next module."
- **The platform story.** If Engage is in scope, remind them that the data infrastructure built for Secure is reusable across Engage and Incident. Expanding module coverage isn't just adding a feature — it's deepening the platform investment.
