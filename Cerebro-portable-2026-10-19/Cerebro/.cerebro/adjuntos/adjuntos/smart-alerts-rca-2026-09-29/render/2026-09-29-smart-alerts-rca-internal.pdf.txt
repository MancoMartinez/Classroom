Root Cause Analysis
Confirmed mechanism, documented in ENG-9486: PR 57544 for ENG-7181 introduced targetFramework="4.8" in the ASP.NET 
httpRuntime configuration around September 24. This enabled the modern ASP.NET synchronization context. 
SendGridEmail.vb awaited the provider call without ConfigureAwait(False), capturing that context for its continuation. 
SmartAlert.vb synchronously blocked the request thread through GetAwaiter().GetResult(). The continuation could not re-
enter the blocked context, producing a deadlock. [S1]
The provider could dispatch the email before the deadlock. Application execution then failed to reach 13b and the 
SmartAlertProcess insert, and IIS could return HTTP 500. The fix allows both continuations to resume without capturing the 
blocked context. It does not serialize alert runs or solve an ADUSA capacity limit. Earlier internal and customer explanations 
that described those mechanisms require correction. [S1–S3, S6]
Related findings require separate tracking: oversized emails, malformed recipient addresses, duplicate recipients and 
incorrect assumptions about alert filters. They do not explain the shared post-release deadlock. The September 20 case lacks 
sufficient retained provider evidence to establish its exact cause. [S3, S4]
