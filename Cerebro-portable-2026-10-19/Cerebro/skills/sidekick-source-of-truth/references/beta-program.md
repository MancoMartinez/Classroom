## Beta survey results

- **Respondents:** 5 (Emma Gomez/Albertsons named; Randy Snyder/Tractor Supply named; 3 anonymous)
- **NPS (0–10):** 3, 5, 8, 8, 9 — average 6.6
- **Overall experience (1–5):** 5, 4, 4, 4, 4 — average 4.2
- **Purchase advocacy:** 1 Definitely / 1 Likely / 2 Unsure / 1 Unlikely
- **Hours saved/week:** "1–2" to "3–4"; two unable to quantify

### Capability ranking (most to least valuable)
1. Search Composer ⭐ (clear winner — #1 for 3 of 4 respondents)
2. Results Analyzer
3. Transaction Lookups
4. General Search & Discovery
5. Store & Cashier Profile Search
6. Lookup & Action of Work Items/Cases/Investigations (weakest — "never worked" per one respondent)

### What's working
- Search Composer — speeds up adding fields, helps users test alternate query approaches
- Strong fit for users learning query writing or not yet proficient
- Results Analyzer well-received on already-built reports
- Plain-language explanations of root-cause issues easy to understand
- Fraud scheme explanations useful for building new reporting

### What needs work (with product responses)
- **Accuracy in Composer** — inaccurate/invalid results flagged
  - *Product response:* Admin-Level Memory (arriving May 29) lets users set field preferences so Sidekick's response is always what they want.
- **Work Items / Cases / Investigations** — consistently weakest; one respondent says "never worked"
  - *Product response:* Testing ongoing; expanding tools available (actions, summaries) to make this more robust cross-product.
- **Complex query behavior** — Sidekick removes non-negotiable Conditions instead of asking first
  - *Product response:* Admin-Level Memory will help; ticket created to support more intelligent composer swaps and lean toward clarifications instead of assumptions.

### Notable individual responses
**Emma Gomez, Albertsons (NPS 9):** "For users that are not strong at question writing Sidekick makes it much easier for them to find what they are looking for." Purchase advocacy: Definitely. Would buy. Most important fix: allow enterprise to grant access based on user not just role.

**Randy Snyder, Tractor Supply (NPS 8):** "Although Sidekick is in its early stages, it is clear that as it evolves it will provide much improved efficiency and ease of use." Purchase: Unsure. Most important fix: don't require Composer view to be open when asking to create/modify a search.

**Anonymous (NPS 3):** "We have a dedicated person on our team whose function is essentially what Sidekick does." Their team already has a dedicated query writer, so the value prop didn't land as strongly. Hours saved: 1–2/week.

### Key insight from feedback
The two strongest advocates (NPS 8 and 9) both point at the same value proposition: Sidekick helps users who aren't strong at query writing. The skeptical respondent (NPS 3) explicitly noted their team already has a dedicated person for that function. This is a segmentation signal — lead with Sidekick's value for teams without a dedicated analyst, and reframe for teams that do have one.
