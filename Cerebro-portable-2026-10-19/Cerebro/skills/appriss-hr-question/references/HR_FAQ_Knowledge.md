## General HR / Profile & Employment Info

**How do I update my personal information?**
Log into Rippling → click your company name or profile icon (top right) → My Profile → Personal Information tab.

**Where can I find my job title or salary?**
Both are on your Rippling profile.

**How do I request an employment verification letter (US & Canada)?**
Direct the verifier to The Work Number (equifax.com/the-work-number). For more complex verification requests, contact People Ops.

**How do I request an employment verification letter (UK)?**
Email your People Operations Manager to request a reference. Specify who it's for and whether any particular information needs to be included.

**Is my HSA investable?**
Yes—if you’re enrolled in an HSA through Rippling Flex, you can open an HSA investment account and start investing (your first investment must be at least $100.00). Set up HSA investing (employee steps)

1. In Rippling, go to Benefits → My Benefits → open your HSA.
2. Click Log in to your HSA account to open the Rippling Flex HSA portal.
3. In the HSA portal, go to Funds Management → Investments.
4. Choose an investment model:

- Managed (guided portfolio based on an intake questionnaire; includes an annual fee)
- Self-directed (you choose funds from a pre-set list)
5. Follow the prompts to complete the questionnaire/terms and transfer money to fund your investments.

### Optional: Enable automatic investing

1. In the HSA portal, go to Manage investments → Get Started.
2. Set the cash balance threshold you want to keep liquid.
3. Set your fund allocation.
4. Click Activate (you can pause/modify/deactivate later).

About these results:

- Eligibility can depend on where the account holder resides (some countries are excluded due to US sanction laws).
- Even though there’s no minimum HSA balance required to begin investing, the initial investment amount must meet the minimum shown above.

## Recruitment

**How do I request a new hire or backfill?**
Review the [hiring process steps form](https://trebu.sharepoint.com/:b:/s/ApprissRetail/Intranet-HR/IQBG7RiHjXOwQq4MM0tvbtoXASxwonn2YrgDjfZGeKI8Vx4?e=aLyy6V) on the intranet. 

**How long does hiring typically take?**
Plan for 4–8 weeks from approved requisition to accepted offer. Senior roles may take longer.

**Who approves a new hire or backfill before posting?**
Your ELT member, the CFO, and regional People Ops managers.

**What's the difference between the Hiring Request and Job Posting Form?**
The Hiring Request initiates the approval process. Once approved, you'll receive the Job Posting Form to fill in role specifics (title, responsibilities, requirements, etc.).

**Can I start interviewing before the req is approved?**
No. The requisition must be fully approved before any posting or formal interviewing begins.

**Can I request a specific job board or recruiter?**
Yes — contact People Ops to discuss options.

**How do I navigate Rippling Recruiting?**
Use the [intranet guide](https://trebu.sharepoint.com/:b:/s/ApprissRetail/Intranet-HR/IQC7mX4Dh4nZQJCjXBuPnqKmARNQzGsJzRpYndVRDdHz7K4?e=pEtht7), or reach out to People Ops for a walkthrough. 

**Are there interview tips or training resources?**
Yes. Use the Candidate Evaluation Form and Behavioral Interview Questions in the [Interview Resources folder](https://trebu.sharepoint.com/sites/ApprissRetail/Intranet-HR/Learning%20Resources/Forms/AllItems.aspx?id=%2Fsites%2FApprissRetail%2FIntranet%2DHR%2FLearning%20Resources%2FHiring%20Resources%2FInterview%20Resources&viewid=4f051219%2Dbe0b%2D4abd%2D91f3%2Dbc6c526e8b3c). These keep interviews structured, consistent, and bias-free. 

**Can I hire a contractor?**
Yes, but approval is required. Contact People Ops before engaging any contractor. Worker misclassification is a significant legal and financial risk — People Ops will help determine the right classification and set up the engagement correctly.

**What's the difference between a contractor and an employee?**
Contractors are typically engaged for a specific scope of work, set their own hours, and aren't entitled to employee benefits. Employees are integrated into the business, directed by the company, and entitled to statutory benefits. If you're unsure, contact People Ops before making any commitments.

**I can't see the jobs that Appriss has listed on our career site. What am I doing wrong?**
Ensure that your cookies are enabled to view jobs on the Appriss Retail career website.

Notify your manager and submit written notice to a member of the People Operations team. Exit procedures will follow.

Timing is governed by the employee's state or country of employment — requirements vary by jurisdiction. People Ops and Payroll will ensure compliance. Contact People Ops with specific questions.

## Benefits & Offboarding FAQ

**When do my benefits end after separation?**
Medical, dental, and vision coverage end on the **last day of your termination month**. All other benefits (life, AD&D, disability, Dependent Care FSA) end on your **last day**.

**How do I enroll in COBRA?**
Rippling will email you enrollment instructions after your last day. You have **60 days from the first of the month following your termination** to enroll (e.g., last day May 3 → 60 days from June 1). Coverage is retroactive, so there's no gap. **Enroll via:** Rippling → Benefits Enrollment → COBRA

**How do I cancel COBRA?**
You must cancel yourself — admins can't do it for you.

**To cancel:** Rippling → Benefits → My Benefits → Current Benefits → click the arrow next to COBRA → Cancel COBRA sub-tab → red **Cancel COBRA** button
> Cancellations only take effect on the **first of a future month**, so cancel by the last day of the month to avoid being charged for the next.

**What happens to my FSA after I leave?**
- **Healthcare and Limited Purpose FSAs** can be continued via COBRA if you have a balance. You have **90 days from your last day** to submit claims for eligible expenses.
- **Dependent Care FSA** is not COBRA-eligible and ends on your last day.

**What happens to my HSA after I leave?**
Your HSA funds are always yours. Within **4–7 business days**, your account converts to a personal UMB Bank / Elevate account. Watch for a setup email from [no-reply@elevate.inc](mailto:no-reply@elevate.inc) (check spam).
> Note: a **$3/month fee** applies after separation.

**What happens to my 401(k) after I leave?**
Your 401(k) is fully vested — it's yours. Fidelity will mail rollover instructions.
- **Phone:** 1-800-835-5095
- **Online:** [netbenefits.com](https://www.netbenefits.com)

**How do I return my equipment?**
Trace3 will ship you a prepaid FedEx return box. Pack your **laptop** (in bubble wrap), **charger(s)**, **monitor(s)**, **keyboard**, **mouse**, and any other Appriss peripherals. Affix the FedEx label over the original and drop off at any FedEx location.
**Questions?** 928-272-4889 or [ithd@trebuco.atlassian.net](mailto:ithd@trebuco.atlassian.net)

**How do I get employment verification after leaving?**
Direct all verification requests (with written authorization) to [hr@apprissretail.com](mailto:hr@apprissretail.com).

**Where can I find my pay stubs and W-2 after I leave?**
Access them via **Rippling using your personal email**. Keep your mailing address current in Rippling so W-2s reach you.

**What should I (a manager) do if my employee tell me they're leaving?**
1. Get written notice
Ask the employee to submit their resignation in writing (email is fine). Loop in People Ops — send it to ddavis@apprissretail.com to kick off the offboarding process.

2. Confirm last day
Nail down their last day so payroll, IT, and benefits timelines can be set.

3. People Ops will handle:
Scheduling an exit interview (confidential, managers aren't present)
Final paycheck timing (varies by state)
COBRA enrollment instructions via Rippling
Equipment return — Trace3 will ship a prepaid FedEx box for their laptop, charger, monitors, etc.

4. Benefits end dates to know:
Medical, dental, vision end on the last day of their termination month
All other benefits (life, AD&D, disability, EAP) end on their last day

5. On your end — start thinking about coverage/backfill and document any knowledge transfer needed before they go. Let People Ops know if you'd like access to their email inbox or OneDrive and who their OOO message should be directed to.

## Performance & Development

**How does the performance review process work?**
Formal reviews with compensation changes happen once a year (January–April). A lighter review cycle runs August–September. Regular 1:1s should happen frequently throughout the year.

**How are bonuses determined?**
Bonuses are split between company performance and personal performance, weighted based on your role and level. Both components are evaluated based on the prior review year.

**Are there career development resources?**
Yes — see the Learning Center.

**Are there internal job postings?**
Yes. The Internal Job Board is accessible to all employees and often mirrors external postings. Talk with your manager about your career goals to stay informed about relevant opportunities. https://app.rippling.com/ats/internal-job-board

**How do I express interest in moving to another team?**
1. Review internal job postings on the HR intranet's Career Page
2. Have an open conversation with your current manager about your goals
3. Reach out to the hiring manager to ask questions and express interest
4. Apply formally through the internal system
5. Expect coordination between teams for a smooth transition

**How do I make an employee referral?**
Submit the [referral form](https://forms.office.com/r/kMSMJvufLv). People Ops will follow up if more details are needed. 

**Someone on my team is underperforming. What do I do?**
Start with a direct, documented conversation with the employee. Loop in People Ops early — don't wait until the issue is severe. People Ops will help assess the situation, determine next steps, and ensure the process is handled consistently and compliantly.

**How do I initiate a PIP?**
Contact People Ops before taking any formal action. People Ops will help determine whether a PIP is the right tool, assist in drafting the plan, and guide the process. A PIP should never be initiated without People Ops involvement.

Three options:
1. **Your manager (preferred first step)** — for most concerns including interpersonal issues, team dynamics, or general policy questions. Managers are expected to receive concerns confidentially and escalate to People Ops when needed.
