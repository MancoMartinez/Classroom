## The Three Questions That Expose Competitors

1. Does your AI resolve customer identities across multiple cards, emails, and addresses — in-store AND online?
2. Does your AI make decisions — or is it analytics that requires your team to decide?
3. Can you query your data AND have it take actions in your workflow?

Most competitors pass one at best.
