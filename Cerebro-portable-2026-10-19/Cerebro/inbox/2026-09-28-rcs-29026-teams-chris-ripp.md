Smart Alert emails were going out, but the process got stuck right after the send step because of a deadlock under load. Nothing was saved to SmartAlertProcess, so Smart Alert History showed nothing since 9/24 and each case stayed flagged as in process, which blocked its later alerts. Kiran's fix (PR 58273) is a one line change that makes the process wait for the previous run to finish. It's working in staging and is being deployed to US today. ADUSA and Vitamin Shoppe show the same pattern.
