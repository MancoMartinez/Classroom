Access to Incident case records is controlled by incident type. Beverly and Francesca are not granted on the incident types where the Peters records sit, so their search returns nothing instead of an error.

Your administrators can fix this two ways. Grant Corporate User and Location Manager on the relevant incident types under Manage Case Type. That opens those incident types to everyone holding those two profiles, not only Beverly and Francesca. Or create a new security level under Manage Security, assign it to Beverly and Francesca only, and grant that level on the incident types they need.
