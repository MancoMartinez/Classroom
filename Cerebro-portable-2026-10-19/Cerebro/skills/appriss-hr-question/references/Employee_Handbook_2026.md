## 2. Code of Conduct

Our purpose, values, and commitment to ethical behavior guide how we work and how we show up for each other and our customers. This Code of Conduct outlines the standards we expect from every employee and helps you make sound decisions when situations aren't clear. It should be used alongside your judgment, common sense, and all other Company policies.

*Nothing in this Handbook creates a contract of employment or alters the nature of employment. Employment with Appriss Retail may be terminated at any time, with or without cause or notice, subject to applicable country law.*

### Using the Code

While we can't list every scenario, the Code provides principles to help you decide the right course of action. When you're unsure, ask yourself:

- Is it lawful and aligned with the spirit—not just the letter—of the law?
- Is it consistent with Company values and policies?
- Would I feel comfortable if this action were public?
- How would my team, family, or friends view this decision?
- Could it negatively affect the Company or others?

To help you better understand and apply the values outlined in our Code of Conduct, you may also refer to our Information Security Policies and Employment Policies (US & UK).

If you can't determine the answer, reach out to your manager, Legal, or People Operations. If you don't feel comfortable raising a concern internally, our confidential Ethics & Compliance Hotline is always available.

### Who Must Follow the Code

The Code applies to every employee, regardless of seniority or role, as well as members of the Board of Directors. Leaders have an added responsibility to model ethical behavior, create space for open dialogue, and act quickly when potential misconduct is identified. Any exception or waiver for a leader must be approved by the Board and disclosed if required by law.

### Voicing Concerns / Whistleblowing

We expect employees to raise concerns about suspected violations of the law, this Code, or Company policies. If you **SEE IT**, we encourage you to **SAY IT**.

Start with your manager when possible. If not, contact Legal or People Operations. If you prefer to report anonymously, you can use the Ethics & Compliance Hotline (US: 888-809-9886, UK: 0808-271-1566) or submit a report online via Red Flag Reporting.

Reported concerns are handled discreetly and appropriately. An investigation will begin based on the nature and urgency of the issue.

New York State DHR hotline – 1-800-HARASS-3

### Protection from Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who raises a concern in good faith—or discouraging others from reporting—is strictly prohibited. The Company will enforce this protection to maintain a safe and open reporting environment.

### Do What's Right

Doing What's Right is our baseline. It means:

- Following all applicable laws
- Upholding our policies
- Being truthful in all interactions
- Treating others with respect
- Protecting our reputation and customer trust

Some legal violations may limit our ability to grant employees access to certain data, which could affect employment. Because of the nature of our work, accuracy, integrity, and honesty are non-negotiable.

### Protecting Our Reputation on Social Media

When you participate on social media, you represent yourself—and the Company. Even if you don't mention work, your online presence reflects our values. Use good judgment, be respectful, and avoid content that could be discriminatory, harmful, or inconsistent with our values. We do not tolerate bigotry or harassment on any social media platform associated with our employees or brand.

This policy does not restrict lawful off-duty conduct or protected activity under applicable labor laws.

If you see content online that could damage the Company or our employees, notify People Operations immediately.

### No Expectation of Confidentiality or Privacy

Company technology, systems, and physical workspaces are Company property. In certain circumstances, the Company may need to access, monitor, or disclose the contents of corporate email, voicemail, computer files, and documents stored on Company devices or in Company offices. This may occur to comply with legal obligations or meet legitimate business needs. Employees should not expect personal privacy when using Company systems or equipment.

### Conflicts of Interest

- Do not use your position at Appriss Retail to benefit yourself, family members, or close associates at the Company's expense.
- Disclose any potential conflicts to your manager and Legal/People Ops before they become actual conflicts.
- Key contacts for conflicts of interest: Vishal Patel, CTO (vpatel@apprissretail.com); Kellie Beckman, General Counsel (kbeckman@apprissretail.com)
- Do not enter into verbal or written agreements on behalf of the Company. All contracts must be in writing and approved by Legal and Information Security before execution by an authorized officer.
- Spend Company money prudently. Responsible financial stewardship helps us continue investing in our people, our customers, and our business.

## 3. Global Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption

Appriss Retail is committed to complying with all applicable anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws, including the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and the UK Bribery Act.

**Key prohibitions:**
- Never offer, pay, promise, or authorize anything of value to government officials, political parties, or candidates to obtain or retain business
- Never accept bribes or kickbacks from any party
- All gifts, entertainment, and travel for third parties must be reasonable, transparent, and properly documented

**Penalties for FCPA violations** include criminal fines, civil penalties, debarment from U.S. government contracts, and denial of export licenses.

### Training

Appriss Retail conducts yearly anti-corruption training for all employees and contractors. Failure to participate may result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

### Accountability

- The Board of Directors has ultimate responsibility for anti-corruption compliance
- The General Counsel and People team administer the compliance program
- Each director, officer, employee, and agent is responsible for complying with Applicable Anti-Corruption Laws, participating in required training, and reporting red flags

### Reporting Violations

If faced with a situation that may violate this policy, immediately notify your supervisor or the Legal Department. Anonymous reports can be made via the Appriss Compliance Hotline: 888-809-9886 or online at https://portal3.redflagreporting.com/appriss-landing-page/.

### Personal Safety Payments

A personal safety payment (payment to avoid imminent physical harm) is permissible. Any employee who reasonably makes such a payment will not be subject to discipline if reported to Legal as soon as possible. Appriss Retail will not reimburse such payments.

## 4. Discrimination, Harassment & Retaliation Prevention Policy

### Summary

The Company is committed to a work environment free from harassment or unprofessional conduct, in which all individuals are treated with respect. This policy applies to everyone involved in the operation of the Company.

### Details

The Company prohibits harassment, disrespectful or unprofessional conduct on the basis of sex (including pregnancy, childbirth, breastfeeding), race, religion, creed, color, gender identity and expression, national origin, physical or mental disability, medical condition, genetic information, marital status, domestic violence victim status, age, sexual orientation, military and veteran status, or any other basis protected by federal, state, or local law.

Examples of prohibited harassing conduct include:

- Offensive or degrading comments related to a protected classification
- Displaying offensive cartoons or telling offensive jokes
- Unwelcome sexual advances or requests for sexual favors
- Retaliation for reporting or threatening to report harassment or discrimination
- Communication via electronic media that includes prohibited conduct

Any employee found to have engaged in unlawful harassment is subject to disciplinary action up to and including termination. Managers who knew about conduct and took no action may be held personally liable.

### Non-Discrimination Based on Pay

Pay discrimination between employees of opposite sexes performing substantially similar work is prohibited. Employees will not be retaliated against for inquiring about or discussing wages.

### Prohibition on Abusive Conduct / Bullying

Abusive conduct (bullying) refers to malicious workplace conduct that a reasonable person would find hostile, offensive, and unrelated to legitimate business interests. This includes repeated verbal abuse, derogatory remarks, insults, and similar conduct.

### Reporting

Report concerns to your manager, People Operations, or the Ethics & Compliance Hotline. All reports are investigated promptly and confidentially.

## 8. Remote First Work Policy

### Overview and Purpose

We operate as a Remote First organization. Performance, impact, and results—not physical location—are the primary focus. Remote work is a privilege, not an entitlement, and may be adjusted based on performance, role requirements, or operational needs.

### Eligibility

All employees who plan to work from home or any other location outside of the formal workplace, either part-time or full-time.

### General Expectations and Conditions

**Rules:** All Company policies continue to apply regardless of location. Disciplinary actions will follow policy transgressions.

**Availability for travel:** Remote employees must be available to travel when required, including to the primary worksite. Employees may be required to work in-office on a regular basis per manager discretion.

**Equipment:** Appriss Retail supplies hardware (laptop, monitors, keyboard, mouse, headset) and software as needed. Equipment is for business purposes only and must be returned upon termination.

**Security:** Keep equipment password protected. Store in a safe location. Follow all data encryption and protection standards. Do not download suspicious, unauthorized, or illegal software.

**Safety:** Maintain a safe home workspace. Remote work is not a replacement for childcare.

**Work expectations:**
- Maintain adequate internet connection
- Dedicate full attention to job duties during working hours
- Adhere to agreed break and attendance schedules
- Ensure schedule overlap with team members
- Be online and accessible for 8 hours per day, Monday to Friday
- Answer correspondence as quickly as possible

**Compensation:** No changes to salary for working remotely. Promotions and skills development remain available.

**Non-exempt employees** must accurately record all hours in Rippling.

Fully Remote employees required to attend mandatory office events will be reimbursed for travel. Hybrid employees do not qualify for this reimbursement.

## 10. Travel and Entertainment Policy

### Summary

This policy provides guidance to all Appriss Retail employees across all regions on responsible travel and entertainment spending when conducting Company business.

**Navan** is the preferred booking and expense platform and should be used whenever possible.

### Authorization and Scope

Applies to all employees and contractors engaged in business activities on behalf of the Company.

Corporate credit cards may be issued to: Managers, Directors, and Executives; regular travelers (AEs, CSMs, etc.); Marketing personnel; exceptions considered upon manager request.

### Travel Expenses

**Airfare:**
- Book at least 10 days in advance
- Flights under 6 consecutive hours: coach/economy/business class; fare not to exceed $1,000 / £800 GBP / $1,350 CAD without pre-approval
- Flights over 6 consecutive hours: business class (not first class) up to $3,000 / £2,400 GBP / $4,000 CAD with manager approval

**Rental vehicles:**
- Intermediate vehicle or smaller; max $119 / £119 GBP / $119 CAD per day
- Book at least 7 days in advance
- Include Vehicle Damage insurance

**Personal automobile:**
- Reimbursed at current IRS mileage rate (US) or HMRC rate (UK)
- Must have valid driver's license and personal auto insurance

**Lodging:**
- Reimbursed when authorized travel is 50+ miles from home or primary worksite
- Reasonable hotel charge: under $350 / £350 GBP / $350 CAD (higher rates acceptable in expensive metro cities with prudent judgment)
- Book through Navan

**Meals:**
- Total meal expense per day should not exceed $100 / £100 GBP / $100 CAD per person
- Alcohol included in per diem maximum; responsible use required

**Parking:** Reasonable fees reimbursable; receipts required

**Tips:** No more than 20% of total meal costs

**Miscellaneous transportation:** Taxi, Uber/Lyft, bus, subway, ferry — receipts required

**Conference registration fees:** Reimbursable with manager approval

### Receipts

- Food and beverage bills > $50.00 / £40 GBP / $65 CAD: receipt required
- Cash payments > $25.00 / £20 GBP / $35 CAD: receipt required
- All hotel folios and itemized rental car receipts: always required
- Must submit itemized receipt plus credit card receipt with signature; names of all attendees required

### In-Office Travel Reimbursement

Employees may be reimbursed for travel to an office location when:
- Requested by manager or Company leadership to attend an in-person meeting/event
- Primary residence is more than 50 miles (one way) from the designated office (US only)
- Visit is business-related and not initiated solely at employee's discretion

**What's covered:** Mileage/transit, airfare/train, lodging (if overnight required or travel > 50 miles), meals per diem. All lodging must be pre-approved by manager, Finance, and People Ops.

### Non-Reimbursable Expenses

- Airline club memberships
- Airline upgrades or first class fare
- Childcare, babysitting, pet-sitting
- Commuting between home and primary work location
- Evening or formal wear
- Haircuts and personal grooming
- Laundry/dry cleaning (unless extended trip, pre-approved)
- Personal entertainment (in-flight movies, health club, pay-per-view, etc.)
- Travel accident insurance premiums or additional travel insurance
- Costs incurred by failure to cancel reservations timely

### Vacation in Conjunction with Business Travel

Employees adding vacation days to a business trip must obtain manager approval prior to booking. Any additional costs from the personal portion must be covered by the employee.

### Travel for Non-Employees

Costs for spouses or family members will not be paid by the Company.

### Responsibilities

**Managers:** Review and approve all expense transactions within 5 days. Failure to accurately review may result in disciplinary action.

**Employees:** Submit expenses promptly and accurately; attach all required documentation; use Company resources responsibly. Employees who do not return funds for personal charges within 30 days will have their card suspended and potentially revoked.

### Hybrid vs. Remote

This policy applies regardless of whether the employee is classified as hybrid, fully remote, or office-based. The focus is on travel distance, not employment classification.

### Policy Compliance

Submitting inaccurate, late, or non-compliant expenses may result in denied reimbursement, card suspension, or disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 11. Charity Work Time Off Policy

### Purpose

Appriss Retail allows one (1) day of paid leave per calendar year to undertake voluntary work with a registered UK, US, or Canadian charity.

### Scope

Applies to all permanent, full-time, and part-time employees in the UK, US, or Canada. Contractors, agency workers, and temporary staff are not eligible.

### Eligibility

All permanent employees who have completed their probationary period. The charity must be a registered US, UK, or Canadian charity (with a valid registration number).

### Conditions of Approval

The day must be approved in advance by your line manager. You must provide:
- The charity's name and registered charity number
- Contact details for a named representative at the charity
- A brief description of the planned activity
- The proposed date

The Company reserves the right to decline requests for charities whose objectives conflict with Company values or policies.

### Payment

- The day does not count against annual leave entitlement
- The charity work day cannot be carried forward to the next calendar year and has no cash value if unused

### Verification and Follow-Up

The Company may contact the named charity representative to verify participation. After the day, employees may be asked to provide a short written summary (what they did, which charity, how it contributed). These details may be shared in internal newsletters or social media — notify People Operations if you do not wish your details shared.

### Administration

Requests should use the standard leave request process, marked as "Charity Work Day." This benefit is non-contractual and can be removed by Appriss Retail at any time.
