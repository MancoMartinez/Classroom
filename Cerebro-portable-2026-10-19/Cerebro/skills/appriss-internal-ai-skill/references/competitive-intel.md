# Competitive Intelligence Reference

The goal is clarity — not disparagement. Use specifics, not generalities. Every comparison below is factual and accurate.

## The Competitive Landscape

Appriss competes across four categories:

| Category | Key Competitors | Their Gap |
|----------|-----------------|-----------|
| Returns Management | Narvar, Optoro, Loop, parcelLab, Claimlane | Rules-based, digital-only; no ML; no in-store visibility; no consumer identity resolution; no consortium intelligence; no real-time decisioning |
| Shrink Management | Agilence, Zebra, Solink | Analytics-focused, not decisioning; AI recently added with limited training data; no cross-retailer consortium; high manual workload |
| CNP / Ecomm Fraud | Forter, Riskified, Signifyd, Accertify | Pre-transaction payment fraud only; returns/claims are rules-based; no in-store behavioral visibility; no consumer identity resolution |
| Case Management | ThinkLP, Auror | Not intelligence-driven; no AI-powered case linking; workflow-heavy, insight-light; AI added recently, not purpose-built |

**Most threatening / most frequently encountered:** Narvar, Agilence, Forter, ThinkLP, Riskified/Signifyd/Accertify, Auror

## vs. Narvar / parcelLab (Returns Experience)

**What they say:** CX-first returns platforms — branded experience, customer communication, and loyalty.

**Their limitation:**
- Rules-based logic. No machine learning.
- Digital-only — zero in-store visibility.
- No consumer identity resolution. No consortium intelligence.
- They only see the online return — not the in-store return from the same consumer 3 days earlier.

**Your edge:**
> "Narvar handles the customer-facing returns experience — portal, shipping labels, routing. That's a different job. But their AI is rules-based. No machine learning. And focused on digital only. If a consumer returns in-store on Monday and files an online claim on Wednesday, Narvar only sees Wednesday. We see both, resolve the identity, and score the full picture."

**The Narvar summary:** They only see Wednesday. We see the whole week — and know who the person is.

---

## vs. Agilence (Shrink / EBR)

**What they say:** Smarter LP platform — EBR, case management, and audit execution.

**Their limitation:**
- Analytics-only. Surfaces insights but stops short of real-time decisions.
- AI recently added — lacks 20+ years training data.
- No consumer identity resolution. No cross-retailer consortium intelligence.

**Your edge:**
> "Agilence is an analytics solution — it surfaces insights. Our AI takes actions. Return decisions, case linking, work item assignment, investigation reports — those are operational outputs, not dashboards. Agilence shows you what happened. Our AI decides what happens next."

**Key differentiators vs. Agilence:**
- Return authorization (Agilence has none)
- Cross-retailer consortium intelligence (Agilence has none)
- Consumer identity resolution (Agilence has none)
- 12+ AI systems live vs. AI recently and partially added

## vs. Forter (CNP / Digital Fraud)

**What they say:** Real-time automated fraud decisions that block fraud without blocking customers.

**Their limitation:**
- Pre-transaction payment fraud only.
- Returns and claims are rules-based, not ML.
- No in-store behavioral visibility.
- If a consumer returns in-store Monday and files a claim online Wednesday — Forter only sees Wednesday.

**Your edge:**
> "Forter protects the checkout. We protect the entire customer lifecycle — in-store, online, returns, claims, and incidents. Our Consumer Identity Resolution connects behavior across every card, email, and channel. Intelligence from 250M IDs reveals patterns Forter can't see."

---

## vs. Riskified / Signifyd / Accertify (Ecommerce CNP Fraud)

**What they say:** AI-driven checkout fraud platforms that shift financial risk and reduce manual review.

**Their limitation:**
- Focused on checkout authorization and chargeback protection.
- Returns and claims are rules-based; no in-store behavioral visibility.
- Essentially an insurance-style risk transfer model.

**Your edge:**
> "We're the system of record for total retail loss — omnichannel insight and full-lifecycle customer behavior analysis vs. insurance-style risk transfer. We stop the fraud before it becomes a chargeback."

---

## vs. ThinkLP (Case Management)

**What they say:** Loss and safety intelligence platform — brings incident, audit, location, and POS data together.

**Their limitation:**
- Built on Salesforce — a case management tool, not a total loss platform.
- No real-time AI decisioning. No return authorization. No consumer identity resolution.
- Requires a dedicated Salesforce Admin.

**Your edge:**
> "ThinkLP is built on Salesforce — it's a case management system, so it addresses one component of total loss. We address total retail loss across all aspects of returns and shrink, with AI making real-time decisions throughout. Enterprise-grade EBR, Incident, Audit, and real-time AI decisioning out of the box — no Salesforce dependency, no per-seat fees, no external admins. AI helps make decisions — ThinkLP surfaces data for humans to act on."

---

## vs. Auror (ORC Intelligence / Case Management)

**What they say:** Retail Crime Intelligence platform focused on reducing crime through shared visibility and collaboration.

**Their limitation:**
- Focuses on incident intake and intelligence sharing — not total retail loss or full-lifecycle behavioral analytics.
- No return authorization, no shrink management, no consumer identity resolution.

**Your edge:**
> "End-to-end behavioral intelligence connecting incidents, returns, claims, and repeat behavior across channels in a single platform. Appriss covers the full loss picture — not just crime incident reporting."

**What Appriss changes:**
