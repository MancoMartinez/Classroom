## Competitive Landscape Overview

The four categories Appriss competes in — and the common gaps across all of them.

| Category | Key Competitors | Problem They Solve | How They Solve It | Common Gaps |
|----------|----------------|-------------------|-------------------|-------------|
| Returns Management | Narvar*, Optoro*, Loop, parcelLab, Claimlane | Returns are expensive, manual, create friction; limited ability to retain revenue or detect abuse. | Branded return portals, return rules & routing, customer self-service, logistics optimization | Rules-based, digital-only; no ML; no in-store visibility; no consumer identity resolution; no consortium intelligence; no real-time decisioning |
| Shrink Management | Agilence*, Zebra, Solink | Growing loss from theft, fraud, operational errors; ORC; need for shrink driver visibility. | EBR from POS data, anomaly dashboards, video integrations, rules/alerts; AI recently added but not purpose-built | Analytics-focused, not decisioning-focused; AI recently added with limited training data; no cross-retailer consortium intelligence; high manual workload |
| CNP / Ecomm Fraud | Forter*, Riskified*, Signifyd*, Accertify | Ecommerce fraud, stolen credentials, fraud sophistication outpaces manual teams. | ML risk scoring, behavioral analytics, bot detection, automated approve/decline, fraud guarantees | Pre-transaction payment fraud only; returns/claims are rules-based, not ML; no in-store behavioral visibility; no consumer identity resolution; false declines hurt revenue |
| Case Management | ThinkLP*, Auror | No consistent processes for investigations, incidents, audits; spreadsheets; no centralized reporting. | Case management workflows, store audits, incident reporting, dashboards, compliance reporting | Not intelligence-driven; no AI-powered case linking; workflow-heavy, insight-light; weak POS/HR integrations; AI added recently, not purpose-built |

*\* Most threatening and frequently encountered competitors.*

**Appriss advantage across all categories:** End-to-end behavioral intelligence connecting incidents, returns, claims, and repeat behavior across channels in a single platform — competitors typically address only one category.

## vs. Status Quo (Rules-Based / Siloed Tools)

| Current State | The Cost | What Appriss Changes |
|--------------|----------|---------------------|
| Separate tools for returns, shrink, and cases. Rules-based policies. Manual review queues. Siloed data across channels. | Good customers get blocked, fraud and abuse slips through. LP team spends hours on manual digging. Finance can't see the full loss picture. No single view of a customer across channels. Millions in margin lost. | Full view of total retail loss and the ability to act on it with extreme accuracy to balance margin, customer experience, and operational efficiency. Real-time customer-level decisions across every channel. One trusted view of every customer and associate. LP investigates more with same headcount. Finance finally sees the full loss picture. |

**Key differentiator:** The status quo often wins by inertia — "we built our own rules." The counter is simple: rules were built on single-retailer data. Appriss decisions are trained on 40% of U.S. retail transactions. No rules engine can replicate that.
