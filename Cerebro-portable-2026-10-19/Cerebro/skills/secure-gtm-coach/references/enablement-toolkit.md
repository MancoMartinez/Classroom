## 1. Account Qualification & ICP

### Ideal Customer Profile

| Dimension | Fit Criteria |
|---|---|
| Revenue | $250M+ annual revenue |
| Format | Has physical store locations with POS transactions (brick-and-mortar required for EBR; omnichannel is ideal) |
| LP Function | Has a dedicated LP team or equivalent that reviews exceptions and investigates cases |
| Shrink Awareness | Shrink is a recognized P&L issue — leadership knows the number and cares about reducing it |
| Data Availability | Can provide POS transaction data; associate/HR data adds significant value |

### Quick-Check: Five Go/No-Go Questions

| Question | Green Light | Red Flag |
|---|---|---|
| Does the retailer have physical stores and $250M+ revenue? | Yes | Under $250M or e-commerce only |
| Is shrink a material, visible cost the LP team is actively trying to address? | Active pain or growing concern | "We manage it fine" or no LP function |
| Can they provide POS transaction data? | Yes, or a clear path to yes | No POS integration possible |
| Is there a functional LP sponsor with budget influence? | Engaged LP leader or exec | No executive interest or LP function is minimal |
| Are they locked into a long-term competitor contract? | Open to evaluation | Signed with Agilence or another vendor in last 6-12 months |

### Red Flags
- No physical stores — EBR is POS-dependent
- No LP function or team to act on exceptions — the value proposition breaks if no one is investigating
- Shrink is not on leadership's radar — no urgency, no champion
- IT has no bandwidth and no workaround for data delivery
- Recently signed a multi-year deal with Agilence — nurture, don't pursue aggressively

## 3. Persona Playbooks

### Loss Prevention VP / SVP
*Owns shrink, fraud, and safety strategy. Reports results to C-suite. Accountable for LP ROI.*

**Pain Points:** Rising shrink with no clear way to decompose it. Pressure to prove LP ROI with hard numbers. Alert overload — too many false positives, not enough investigations that close. Limited cross-functional visibility into inventory shrink and operational loss.

**Value Narrative:** Secure gives you one unified view of risk — across every associate, every store, every category of loss. Instead of reacting to the exceptions your rules generate, you're working from an AI-ranked queue that surfaces what's statistically abnormal. Your team investigates less, closes more, and builds a defensible ROI story for the C-suite.

**Discovery Starters:**
- "How are you currently measuring LP ROI? What does the C-suite ask for?"
- "What percentage of exceptions your current tool generates end up being real cases?"
- "If you had to guess what's driving your shrink number, which categories feel most uncertain?"
- "How do your investigators find cases today? How manual is that process?"

**Objection Handling:**
- *"We already have EBR. Why do we need Secure?"* → What EBR are they using? If rules-based, lean into the intelligence gap: rules find what you tell them to find. Secure finds what's statistically outside the norm — without anyone writing a rule first. Ask: "How many false positives is your team working through per week?"
- *"Shrink reduction is our priority, not a new tool."* → That's exactly why Secure is the conversation. The average customer sees 12% shrink reduction. If their current tool was doing that, they wouldn't need to talk.
- *"We don't have bandwidth to implement another system."* → The implementation lift is front-loaded on data delivery. Once data flows, Secure runs — your team doesn't maintain rules, write thresholds, or tune the system. You configure once and it adapts.

### Loss Prevention Manager
*Day-to-day investigator. Builds cases, manages alerts, coordinates store teams. Closest to the data.*

**Pain Points:** Alert overload — too many exceptions, not enough time. Manual digging through disconnected reports to build a case. Hard to prioritize which exceptions are worth pursuing. Can see individual transactions but can't see patterns across time.

**Value Narrative:** Secure does the triage work for you. AI-ranked exception queues mean your highest-confidence cases are at the top — you're not guessing which alerts matter. Search Composer lets you build queries across cashiers, stores, SKUs, and time periods in seconds instead of hours. Every exception generates a work item you can assign, track, and resolve without switching systems.

**Discovery Starters:**
- "How do you prioritize which exceptions to investigate? Is that manual or system-driven?"
- "Walk me through what a typical investigation looks like. How many systems are you touching?"
- "How many alerts does your current system generate in a week? How many become real cases?"
- "Have you ever missed a pattern that you only caught after the loss had already compounded?"

**Objection Handling:**
- *"We can't add another tool to the workflow."* → Secure replaces the manual digging, not adds to it. The exception queue is ranked for you. Work items are generated automatically. It's designed to reduce the workflow burden, not increase it.
- *"Our investigators are already overwhelmed."* → That's the core problem Secure solves. If your team is overwhelmed, they're working from unranked alerts. Secure cuts investigation time in half by surfacing the cases that matter first.

**Pain Points:** Shrink is a line item that's hard to decompose and harder to attribute to specific causes. New technology spend requires a clear, defensible business case with measurable outcomes.

**Value Narrative:** Secure quantifies the loss that was invisible before — and delivers documented shrink reduction that ties directly to margin improvement. 12% average shrink reduction, 6x ROI, and measurable outcomes tracked from day one.

**Discovery Starters:**
- "How is shrink currently tracked and reported at the leadership level?"
- "What would a 10% reduction in your shrink rate mean to your EBITDA?"
- "What does the approval process look like for a new LP technology investment?"

**Objection Handling:**
- *"We need hard numbers before we can justify the spend."* → That's the right ask. We'll model the business case against your actual transaction data — annual transactions, location count, and current shrink rate. The ROI model is transparent and customer-specific, not a black box.
- *"Our shrink number hasn't changed in years. I'm not sure this moves the needle."* → A flat shrink number that isn't decomposed often means loss is there but undetected, not that it isn't happening. Secure surfaces the signal inside the number. 12% average reduction across the customer base suggests there's typically more to find than the static number implies.

### Operations Leader (Store / Retail Ops)
*Manages processes, training, and consistency across locations. High turnover, time pressure.*

## 4. Objection Handling by Theme

### Theme 1: "We Already Have EBR"
*Underlying assumption: "Our current tool does the same thing."*

**Reframe:** Ask what EBR they're using. The question isn't whether they have exception reporting — it's whether their tool is rules-based or AI-driven. Rules-based tools find what you define. Secure finds what's statistically abnormal, regardless of whether anyone anticipated the pattern. These are fundamentally different approaches.

**Key questions to surface the gap:**
- "How many alerts does your system generate per week? How many become real cases?"
- "When was the last time you updated your rules? Who owns that process?"
- "Have you ever discovered a loss pattern after the fact that your current tool missed?"

**Proof Points:** AI-trained outlier models across 60+ top 100 retailers. 12% shrink reduction. 50% less investigation time.

### Theme 2: ROI Credibility
*Underlying fear: "These numbers feel too good to be true."*

**Reframe:** The 12% shrink reduction and 6x ROI are averages across the Secure customer base. Offer to model against their specific data (annual transactions, location count, current shrink rate) to build a retailer-specific business case. The ROI model is transparent and uses their numbers.

**Proof Points:** Grocery case study ($30M savings). National retailer inventory results (1.83% vendor credit lift). Cash O/S grocery case (single-screen reconciliation replacing multi-query process).

### Theme 3: Implementation / Bandwidth
*Underlying fear: "We don't have capacity to onboard another tool."*

**Reframe:** The implementation lift is front-loaded on data delivery. Once POS, location, product, and employee data flows, Secure runs. There are no rules to write, no thresholds to configure manually, and no model maintenance on the retailer's side. Post-go-live IT involvement is near-zero.

**Key distinction vs. Agilence:** Rules-based tools create ongoing maintenance burden (someone has to keep the rules current). Secure's statistical models adapt automatically.

### Theme 4: Change Management / Associate Pushback
*Underlying fear: "Our store teams won't adopt this."*

**Reframe:** Secure doesn't change what associates do — it changes what LP and managers see. The store-level workflow is unchanged. The LP investigation workflow gets faster and more targeted. Coach gives managers a system-generated conversation starter, not a manual report to build.

## 5. Competitive Landscape

### Agilence

Agilence is the most common named competitor in Secure conversations. Position based on what the customer or prospect values.

**What Agilence does:** Exception-based reporting built on a rules and threshold model. Retailers define what the system looks for. Strong market presence, solid UI, widely adopted.

**Where Secure wins:**

| Capability | Appriss Secure | Agilence |
|---|---|---|
| AI-trained outlier models | Yes — 12+ models | Limited / rules-based |
| Cross-retailer consortium data | 60+ top 100 retailers | No |
| Coach / behavior change module | Yes — native | No |
| Cash Over/Short module | Yes — native | Adjacent (not native) |
| Inventory module | Yes — native | Limited |
| Sidekick AI (embedded) | Yes | No |
| Average shrink reduction | 12% | Varies |

**The core competitive argument:**
1. **Intelligence gap:** Agilence surfaces what you define. Secure surfaces what's abnormal. These are fundamentally different approaches.
2. **Consortium data:** Agilence has no cross-retailer behavioral dataset at this scale. The patterns Secure's models identify come from 60+ top 100 retailers — no single retailer's data would surface them alone.
3. **Maintenance burden:** Agilence rules require ongoing updates when POS flows, promos, or vendor relationships change. That maintenance falls on the retailer. Secure's statistical models adapt automatically.
4. **Sidekick:** Agilence has no embedded AI workflow layer. Secure has a conversational AI assistant built in.

**Critical approach note:** Don't lead by attacking Agilence. Lead with discovery — "How is it working for them? Where do they see gaps?" Then let the gaps in their current tool do the selling. Most Agilence customers have high false positive rates, manual investigation workflows, and rule maintenance headaches. Surface those before positioning Secure.

### Homegrown / Rules-Based Systems

**What they are:** Custom-built exception reporting using SQL queries, manual thresholds, or simple rule engines. Often built by IT on top of existing BI tools.

**The positioning:** Don't attack. Ask questions that surface the cost of the current approach:
- "How much time does your IT team spend maintaining the rules when operations change?"
- "When was the last time you added a new exception rule? How long did it take?"
- "What happens when a new fraud scheme emerges that your rules don't cover?"

**Where Secure wins:**
- Outlier detection finds patterns no rule was written for
- AI models trained on 60+ top 100 retailers' data — no homegrown tool can replicate that
- No ongoing maintenance burden on IT
- Sidekick as a conversational interface eliminates the need for custom BI queries

## 6. Module Deep-Dives

### Exception-Based Reporting (EBR)
*The core module — included in every package tier.*

**What it does:**
- Scores every transaction against AI-trained outlier models — no thresholds to write
- Flags unusual POS and Ecom activity (high voids, suspicious discounts, return patterns)
- Search Composer: custom queries across cashiers, stores, SKUs, and time periods in seconds
- AI Outliers: surfaces associates and stores performing statistically outside the norm
- Every flagged exception generates a work item, investigation, or coaching session in one click

**Use cases:**
Cashier integrity · Sweethearting · Tender manipulation · Refund fraud · Coupon abuse · Discount override · SCO exceptions

**Key differentiator:** Rules-based tools check against thresholds you define. EBR's outlier detection surfaces the statistical anomaly regardless of whether a rule exists for it. The pattern becomes visible before anyone anticipates the scheme.

**Talk track for LP Manager:**
"Search Composer replaces the manual query-building your investigators are doing today. Instead of spending 2 hours pulling data across multiple systems to build a case, they're building the same query in seconds. The AI-ranked queue means the highest-confidence cases are at the top — your team works what matters first."

### Secure Coach
*Most common first module expansion from Essential.*

**What it does:**
- Auto-generates a coaching task when a cashier's metrics cross a configured threshold
- KPIs are fully customizable — pinpoints associates needing correction, not punishment
- Connects to POS, Cash Over/Short, and Inventory data
- Tracks coaching completion and measures performance improvement over time
- Works across all exception types in one workflow

**The core reframe:**
Most employee-driven shrink isn't theft — it's a training problem. Coach turns exception data into documented, structured conversations that change behavior and protect good employees. Without Coach, LP teams see the behavior, escalate to HR, and often lose the employee entirely — when a coaching conversation would have solved it.

**Customer case study (Top Grocery Retailer):**
- $30M in savings via SRA reduction
- 46% decrease in hand-keyed items
- 26% decrease in negative hand-keyed
- 13% decrease in transaction voids
- 10% decrease in coupon application
- 10% decrease in negative sales

**Cost of inaction:**
$5,000+ to recruit and train a replacement associate. Multiply by the number of avoidable terminations in the last year. That's the floor of Coach's value — before counting the behavioral improvement that reduces ongoing loss.

### Cash Over/Short
*Unlocks real-time cash variance visibility by register, cashier, and shift.*

**What it does:**
- Tracks cash drawer variances by store, register, and cashier
- Captures paid-in/out, no-sale, and cash-back
- One-screen Over/Short Summary for corporate and field AP
- Cash Flips tab flags reversals
- Enables investigation drill-down and work item assignment

**The core problem it solves:**
By the time you find a shortage in nightly reconciliation, the decision window is already closed. Cash Over/Short surfaces variance in real time — by the shift, by the register, by the cashier — so teams can act while it's still happening.

**Customer story (National Grocery Retailer):**
Multi-query cash reconciliation replaced with a single-screen Over/Short Summary. Corporate and field AP teams now monitor daily. Operational fixes reduced false positives and freed teams to focus on true theft cases.

**Discovery question:**
"How do you currently track cash variances? Is that happening real-time or next-day? What happens when a shortage is discovered — how far back can you trace it?"

### Secure Inventory
*Connects the receiving dock to the register. Unlocks vendor discrepancy and stockroom visibility.*

**What it does:**
- Tracks receiving, transfers, and vendor deliveries (DSD)
- Detects vendor discrepancies: short-ships, over-ships, recurring errors
- Surfaces stockroom manipulation: more refunds than units sold, on-hand growth with no valid shipment
- When paired with EBR: tells the complete story from dock to register
- 14 out-of-the-box risk searches ready to deploy on day one

**Customer results (National Retailer):**
- 1.83% sustained lift in vendor credits per period
- 0.23% sustained improvement in DSD working capital

**Talk track:**
"EBR sees what happens at the register. Inventory sees what's happening in the back of the house — receiving, transfers, vendor deliveries. When you connect the two, you can see patterns that neither system can surface alone. A refund volume that exceeds units received. An inventory adjustment pattern tied to a specific associate. A DSD vendor with consistent short-ships across multiple locations."

### Sidekick
*Conversational AI collaborator embedded across the Appriss platform.*

**What it does:**
- Ask questions in plain English — no report-building
- Queries exceptions, trends, associate performance, and store data
- Surfaces insights in real time, not on a scheduled report cadence
- Works across Engage, Secure, and Incident — answers span the full loss picture
- Takes action: build and run searches, pull up profiles, assign work items

**Who it resonates with:**
- **LP Manager:** "Ask Sidekick to find every cashier in the district with above-average void rates in the last 30 days. It runs the query and returns the list."
- **LP VP:** "Ask Sidekick for a summary of last week's exception volume by store tier. Gets you a board-ready view in seconds."
- **Ops Leader:** "Ask Sidekick which stores had the highest cash variance last quarter without building a custom report."

**Competitive differentiator:**
Agilence has no embedded AI workflow layer. Sidekick converts any question about exceptions or loss into an action without requiring a BI analyst or custom query. It's the feature that removes the "I need to pull a report" bottleneck from every LP workflow.

| Signal | What It Means | Next Conversation |

| Renewal approaching | Natural expansion moment | Bundle module expansion with renewal |

### The Module Expansion Sequence

**Most common path:** Essential (EBR only) → Pro (add Coach + Inventory Financial Est.) → Add Cash O/S → Add Inventory Ins/Outs & DSD → Add-ons (Video, Extra Retention, Additional Feeds)

**Fastest expansion conversation:** Coach — lowest resistance because it's a behavior change story, not a new investigation paradigm. The ROI is tied to avoided terminations and documented behavior improvement, both of which resonate with LP and Ops simultaneously.

### Cross-Sell Quick Reference

- **Secure → Engage:** Customer has shrink management but isn't managing return fraud and abuse with AI. Signal: returns come up in SBRs or loss discussions. Cross-sell framing: "The data infrastructure you've built for Secure is reusable for Engage. One platform, one unified view of loss across shrink and returns."
- **Secure → Incident:** Customer using Secure data to surface patterns tied to ORC or organized employee theft and needs case management to track and resolve them. Signal: LP team manually managing case files. Cross-sell framing: "The exceptions Secure surfaces need somewhere to go when they become investigations. Incident closes that loop."
- **Engage → Secure:** Customer has returns optimization but not shrink analytics. Signal: LP team using Engage data for investigations and wanting more comprehensive loss visibility. "What about the shrink happening before customers even get to the return counter?"

**CSM comfort note:** You're not selling — you're identifying a gap in their coverage. Phrase it as: "I've noticed X in your SBR data. There's a capability that addresses this directly — want me to loop in your AE for a conversation?"

Implementation lift is front-loaded. Once data feeds are established and validated, IT involvement drops to near-zero. Unlike rules-based tools that require ongoing rule maintenance, Secure's models adapt automatically.

**Q: How is data secured?**
Cloud-hosted, SOC 2 Type II compliant. Supports vendor security reviews. Standard data delivery protocols (API, SFTP). No proprietary hardware or on-premises infrastructure required.

**Q: Can we start with one data source and expand?**
Yes. Start with POS, location, product, and employee — that's the EBR foundation. Cash, inventory, and vendor data unlock modules and add value incrementally.
