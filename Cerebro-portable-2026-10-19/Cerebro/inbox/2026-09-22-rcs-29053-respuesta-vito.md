Cashier 0 is a system generated entry that shows up once for each active register every day, and it is not tied to an actual cashier login. It records a drawer opening that happened without a sale attached to it.

That entry carries no dollar value. The cash tendered, cash back, and change amounts on it are always zero, so it does not add anything to your over short total for the day.

It is meant to stay in the dataset. It is the record of that drawer opening, not a leftover or a duplicate you can filter out.

Let me know if you run into a case where it looks different from what I described, and I will take another look.
