## Accessing the Platform

### How do I log into my recognition account?

Go to [https://apprissretail.awardco.com/welcome](https://apprissretail.awardco.com/welcome) and log in using single sign-on.

### What happens to my points if I leave the organization?

Once an account is archived, any remaining eligible points will be automatically returned to the company's point bank.

### Who can I reach out to if I have questions about the platform, redemptions, previously placed orders, or recognitions?

For any questions related to the platform and redemption, please reach out to the Awardco support team or the Appriss Retail People Team. There are three ways to contact Awardco support:

- **Chat** – Click the blue "Chat" button in the bottom right corner of the page or from any page in your Awardco platform. The chatbot will attempt to resolve your inquiry; if needed, you'll be redirected to a support representative.
- **Email** – Create a support ticket automatically by emailing support@awardco.com. Include as much detail as possible: your full name, company name, and a detailed description of the issue.
- **Phone** – Contact the Awardco support team using the numbers listed at [award.co/contact](https://award.co/contact).

### I do not want to see the whole company's recognition feed. Is there a way I can filter recognitions to only those that are relevant to me?

On the left side of the recognition Feed page, you will see filters that can be selected to isolate the team, business segment, or individual you'd like to see. Consider creating "Saved Views" with these filters for quick access in the future.

## Recognition/Feedback

### How do I begin recognizing my peers?

Log into your account at [https://apprissretail.awardco.com/welcome](https://apprissretail.awardco.com/welcome) and from the homepage, click the **Recognize** tab under the logo in the top left corner of the screen.

### Can I recognize my peers who are in a different country and on a different team?

Yes! In fact, we encourage it. Recognize your peers near and far!

### How do I recognize through Microsoft Teams?

To recognize employees through MS Teams, download the Awardco app in Teams and login to your instance. Our company code is Apprissretail.  

### How do I know when to give someone a monetary Spot award or a non-monetary Spot award?

It's up to you! Depending on your High Five point bank, you have the ability to gift any amount of points to your peers, or recognize them without points. This option will be available on the Recognize screen.

### How do I recognize all members of a project or team at once?

When searching for award recipients, list each recipient on the recipient selection page to create a group recognition. When multiple people are selected as part of the same recognition, they will each receive the same message, tags, images, and award value (where applicable).

### As a manager, will I be able to see recognition from my team all in one place?

Group Managers have access to recognition reporting in the Admin section of their account. To view these reports, log into your account, click the "Admin" button just under your name in the top right corner, and click the "Reports" tab. The Recognition Details report will show all recognitions in which your employees were participants.

### Can managers only nominate someone from their team, or can they nominate other users as well?

Managers can nominate anyone regardless of team. We encourage cross-departmental appreciation!

### What does the approval process look like?

Approval processes are only used for the Life @ Appriss Program and the Wellness Program. In those cases, the People Team will verify task completion.

## Redemption

### How do I redeem my points within the platform?

After logging into your account, click the **Spend Points** tab under the logo in the top left corner of the screen. Hovering over this tab will reveal a drop-down menu listing the available redemption categories. Click any category to see individual redemption options.

### How long do I have to redeem my points?

Points do not expire in the platform. However, points will be revoked on an employee's final day if they are no longer employed with Appriss Retail.

### How do the points work if I am located in a country (i.e. India) where the cost of living is lower than in the United States?

Purchasing power parity (PPP) ratios are enabled. PPP allows employees in multiple countries to receive equitable awards by adjusting the purchasing power of points based on local cost of living. This ensures every individual, regardless of location, receives equivalent purchasing power to purchase the same general "basket of goods." For more information, see [this Awardco support article](https://awardco.my.site.com/Customerhelp/s/article/Purchasing-Power-Parity?language=en_US).

### I do not have enough points to purchase an item. Can I purchase points directly through the recognition platform?

It is not possible to buy additional points for use in the Awardco platform.

### What kinds of things can I redeem my points for?

Available redemption options vary by country. All employees have access to the Amazon catalogue, a variety of gift cards, charitable donations, and more.

### Why can't I see items in Awardco that I can see on Amazon?

To ensure quality and coverage under Amazon's 30-day return policy, only items directly warehoused and fulfilled by Amazon are eligible for redemption in Awardco. Items sold by third-party sellers in the Amazon Marketplace are not available, but this provides better protections when spending your points.

### What are ineligible points?

Participation in the wellness program allows employees to choose the wellness credit (US employees only) or items from the wellness catalogue. These points are ineligible for use with any other redemption option such as gift cards or the company store.

## Programs

### High Five Program

Employees receive points on the 1st of each month through the High Five Program:

- **Individual contributors** – 5 points
- **Managers** – 10 points

Points given to you cannot be re-gifted. Points allocated for recognizing peers expire at the end of the month and do not roll over — be sure to use them before they lapse.

### Life @ Appriss Program

Participate in monthly challenges to earn 5 points. Points cannot be re-gifted.

### Wellness Program

#### US Employees

- **January–December 2026**: Employees have until mid-December (date changes slightly each year) to earn the wellness credit for the following year. Submit various wellness activities for points in the Awardco platform (the widget should be on your home page). Once you've earned 100 points, redeem them for the **Premium Discount**. Be sure to follow all these steps so that People Ops is notified. The 'discount' will show up as a credit on every paycheck as the delta between the regular premium costs and the wellness program premium costs. Example: The regular premium is $100. The wellness premium is $50. You would receive $25 ($50/month) on each paycheck.
- **January–December 2026 (not enrolled in Appriss benefits)**: Employees not enrolled in Appriss Retail benefits can still participate to earn 100 points, redeemable in the wellness catalogue. Spend them as you earn them or save up the full 100 and spend all at once.

#### UK/Canada Employees

Employees can participate in the wellness program to earn 100 points, redeemable in the wellness catalogue. Spend them as you earn them or save up all 100 to spend at once.

#### Points values

| Points | Activities |
|--------|-----------|
| 2 | Calm app activity; 10k steps in one day; Workout; Gym attendance |
| 5 | Cook a healthy meal; Try a new hobby; Journal |
| 20 | Community service/volunteering; Athletic event; CPR/First Aid certification; Blood donation; Annual exam; Dental check-up; Eye exam; Vaccine; League participation |

#### Submission notes

- Activities must be submitted with proof of completion (formatted as an image) via the appropriate Wellness Program widget.
- The wellness premium discount will be applied the following month after it's earned — e.g., if the discount is bought January 5, it will be credited February 1.
