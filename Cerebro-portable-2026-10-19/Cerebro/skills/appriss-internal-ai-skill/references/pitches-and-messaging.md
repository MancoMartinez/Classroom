## AI Framing: 5 Research-Backed Themes
*(Based on March 2026 buyer research — buyer feedback, LinkedIn polls, enterprise customer calls)*

### Theme 1: Lead with data, not model count
**Do:** "Built on 20+ years of retail data and 250M consumer IDs"  
**Don't:** "We have 12 AI models" — model count is table stakes, not differentiation

### Theme 2: Prove it, don't claim it
Half of surveyed buyers are skeptical the moment they hear "AI-powered." Lead with outcomes.  
**Do:** "6x faster root cause identification" / "Declines fraudulent returns before they hit your P&L"  
**Don't:** "Our cutting-edge AI transforms your loss prevention strategy"

### Theme 3: Specificity wins
**Do:** "Behavioral pattern recognition. Real-time risk decisions. Natural language investigations."  
**Don't:** "AI-powered platform" / "Advanced machine learning"

### Theme 4: Position as most mature, not an introduction
75% of buyers are already running multiple AI vendors. They're comparing — not learning what AI is.  
**Do:** Position Appriss as the most differentiated and proven option  
**Don't:** Explain what AI does or treat them as AI newcomers

### Theme 5: Equip the LP champion to sell upward
LP leaders are rarely the final budget holder. Equip them to make the case to CFO, Head of Stores, CEO.
- **For finance:** margin %, EBITDA impact, payback period
- **For ops:** handle time, training burden, process consistency
- **For CEO:** shrink trend, loss as % of sales, brand protection

## The Three Questions to Expose Competitors

Use these in discovery and competitive situations:

1. **"Does your AI resolve customer identities across multiple cards, emails, and addresses — across in-store and online channels?"**
2. **"Does your AI make decisions — or is it primarily analytics-focused and requires your team to make the calls?"**
3. **"Can you query your data to get insights? Are you able to have it take action within your workflow?"**

Most competitors fail at least two of these three.

## Audience-Specific Translations

### LP / Asset Protection Manager
**Lead with:** "Surface the high-value issues faster — so your team investigates what matters, not what the data team eventually gets back to you about. Sidekick answers your toughest questions in seconds."  
**Key pain:** Alert overload, manual digging, siloed data, limited headcount  
**Proof:** 6x faster root cause identification. 50% reduction in investigation time. Any analyst finds what used to require a power user.  
**KPIs they care about:** Cases opened/closed, time to investigation, $ recovered, fraud rings identified

### LP VP / SVP (Strategy Owner)
**Lead with:** "Full visibility into shrink and returns-related loss — addressed faster, so you can prove margin impact, justify the budget, and do more with the team you have."  
**Key pain:** Disconnected systems, difficulty proving ROI, rising loss and violence, no total loss picture  
**Proof:** 60+ top 100 retailers. Total-loss coverage across channels. Exec-level reporting.  
**KPIs they care about:** Shrink %, total loss %, incident rates, fraud impact, cross-channel loss trends

### CFO / VP Finance
**Lead with:** "Every time a fraudulent return hits your P&L or a cashier exception goes undetected, you're buying back loss you already paid for — before your team even knows it happened."  
**Key pain:** Diminishing profits, low visibility into loss sources, competing capital allocation  
**Proof:** Clear payback period. Margin/EBITDA impact. Declines fraudulent returns before P&L impact.  
**KPIs they care about:** Margin %, EBITDA impact, payback period, TCO vs current stack

### CX / Customer Experience Leader
**Lead with:** "Reward your best customers while reducing loss — appreciate the best, mitigate the rest. Our AI knows who your loyal customers are across every channel, so good shoppers never pay the price for someone else's fraud."  
**Key pain:** Friction in returns, fear of losing market share, conflicting KPIs with LP/finance  
**Proof:** Configurable warnings vs. denials. Low insult rate. Reward good customers.  
**KPIs they care about:** NPS, return/denial complaints, escalation volume, warning vs denial outcomes

### CEO / Executive
**Lead with:** "Shrink and returns are your two biggest unmanaged loss vectors. We give you one trusted view of both — and the AI to act on it before it hits earnings."  
**Key pain:** Macro-level risk, earnings volatility, no simple strategic view of total loss  
**KPIs they care about:** Shrink trend, loss as % of sales, revenue at risk protected

### Operations Leader
**Lead with:** "Quickly navigate shrink and returns with minimal coaching — the AI handles the triage, your team handles what matters."  
**Key pain:** High workload, constant turnover, lack of process consistency  
**KPIs they care about:** Queue times, handle time, training time, process adherence

### E-commerce / Digital Leader
**Lead with:** "Accurately action on fraud, abuse, returns, and claims to protect margins while maintaining a stellar experience."  
**Key pain:** High online returns, growing fraud pressure, point solutions with narrow fraud-only mindset  
**KPIs they care about:** Online return rate, claim rate, insult rate, digital margin per order

### CTO / IT Leader
**Lead with:** "Reduce redundancies, satisfy store/ecom/LP/finance without adding IT burden — and an AI governance program you can actually show procurement."  
**Key pain:** Integration complexity, data reconciliation, too many vendors  
**KPIs they care about:** Time to implement, # integrations re-used vs net new

### SVP of Transformation
**Lead with:** "Consolidate vendors, align teams, and show measurable ROI against shrink and returns-related loss without adding another change initiative."  
**Key pain:** Cross-functional complexity, change fatigue, many point solutions  
**KPIs they care about:** Adoption across BUs, platforms consolidated, ROI vs business case
